OCTOBER 12

Grow with Patience

And let us not grow weary while doing good, for in due season we shall reap if we do not lose heart. (Galatians 6:9)

Throughout history, God has moved in ways too powerful for us to even imagine. Yet, for a Christian with understanding, the most significant move of God unfolded in Christ Himself: His incarnation, His exemplary life with the disciples, His trials, death, burial, resurrection, and ascension. Within this complete redemptive work lies the prescribed pattern for spiritual growth. But this growth is not instantaneous. This growth demands patience.

Luke 2:40 discloses the progressive development of the Lord Jesus, stating He “grew in an increasing measure.” In other words, He began at a point and steadily advanced in various areas, including wisdom. Verse 52 of that same chapter reinforces this: “And Jesus kept increasing in wisdom and stature, and in favour with God and men.”

What does this tell you? Growth is always progressive. And because growth is progressive, patience is the essential pathway to it.

Patience is truly the key to consistent and steady growth. Without it, fulfilling God’s design—whether it is your conformity to Christ or the realisation of His promises—becomes impossible (Hebrews 6:11–15).

Consider the human race and all living things: we start out as helpless, dependent infants. Yet, with God’s inherent blueprint and the benefit of time and consistency, growth is inevitable. This natural progression mirrors the path for your spiritual journey toward knowledge, wisdom, understanding, and a mighty stature in the spirit.

Remember, the Scripture boldly reveals that even our Lord Jesus Christ subjected Himself to this growth process, both physically and spiritually. Hebrews 5:8 states that He ‘learned obedience.’ This same principle applies directly to your spiritual journey: there is ample room for learning, provided you embrace patience and consistency.

In conclusion, to grow into the fullness of Christ, patience is non-negotiable. You must intentionally learn to wait on the Lord, which means that you must trust Him for the needed grace and wisdom to attain spiritual capacity and stature. The enduring truth remains: ‘those who wait on the Lord will have their strength renewed’ (Isaiah 40:31).

🙏 PRAYER
Patient Father, You embody perfect patience. Teach me to embrace patience in my own growth journey, so I do not become weary or hasty and thereby hinder Your work within me.
