OCTOBER 16

The Fight of Faith

Fight the good fight of faith, lay hold on eternal life, to which you were also called and have confessed the good confession in the presence of many witnesses. (1 Timothy 6:12)

The believer is at war daily. There is a constant clash between his flesh and his spirit, and the battlefield is the mind.

There is a popular saying that “the flesh is trying so hard to take you to hell but will not be there with you.” It is funny but it is also true. In this matter, the Bible has set its diagnosis: “the desires of the flesh are against the Spirit, and the desires of the Spirit are against the flesh” (Galatians 5:17, ESV). The trophy is eternal life: one side wants to place you in eternal damnation; the other side wants to bring you into God's eternal Kingdom.

To the studious believer, there is nothing unfamiliar about this fight. In the past, it was fought by the great cloud of witnesses mentioned in Hebrews 12. Jesus Christ, our eternal forerunner, also fought this battle and today sits victorious at the right hand of the Father. His apostles also fought the battle and won. Sadly, Judas fought his battle but became a casualty.

The devil knows he only has a short time. So, he is very aggressive in his mission to pull believers of the Lord Jesus into eternal damnation (1 Peter 5:8). He is very subtle. He uses Mammon (material wealth). He also uses what John called “the lust of the flesh, the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life” (1 John 2:16), knowing that every other sin is founded upon these three.

In all of these, the Ancient of Days, our eternal King, charges us to put on His full armour (Ephesians 6:10–17). When the wicked one (and life in this fallen world) throws needs, delays, sickness, distress, and many more at you, remember why you first believed, in Whom you believed, and the end of your faith.

Lay aside the things of the flesh, and the worries tied to this world. There is Someone worth paying more attention to: the Lord Jesus Christ, the author and finisher of your faith. He is your pattern. He overcame so that you might overcome.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Fight the good fight of faith, lay hold on eternal life, to which you were also called and have confessed the good confession in the presence of many witnesses.
(1 Timothy 6:12)
🙏 PRAYER
Good Lord, thank You for the gift of faith, of believing in and trusting in You. Please, I want to lay hold on eternal life. Help me go on having faith and being faithful in all You have called me to be and do.
