OCTOBER 2

The Heartfelt Prayer

When You said, “Seek My face,” my heart said to You, “Your face, Lord, I will seek.” (Psalm 27:8)

For a long time, prayer has been defined as communicating with God. In this manner, we speak to Him and He speaks back to us. Prayer can also be seen as a response to God beckoning on us. It should be committed to memory that how man responds to this beckoning isn’t superficial but from the heart. God is not so much concerned with your outward show of piety as He is with the state of your heart.

Many times, prayers that yield no fruits (even after being prayed for a while) are those done with no heart commitment to them. There is no time the Lord’s search light is not upon your heart, even in the place of prayer. After all, He says He is the Lord who searches your heart and mind (Jeremiah 17:10).

In Matthew 15:8–9, Jesus quotes Isaiah to the people He separated for Himself: “You draw near to Me, yes, but your hearts are far from Me.” It therefore follows that even when Apostle James instructs that you draw near to God (James 4:8), he means that your heart must absolutely be involved.

Yes, prayer isn’t the only manner by which a man can run after God; but it is one of great importance. In the Bible, there are a few notable examples of people who prayed with their hearts and God responded to them. These examples are more about their actions in this context and not necessarily their answers.

Jesus, on the night of His arrest in the garden of Gethsemane, prayed earnestly. Scripture records that it was while He was praying that an angel appeared and strengthened Him (Luke 22:43). With such an effective prayer life, it is no surprise that at one point, the disciples of Jesus asked Him to teach them how to pray.

Hannah prayed from her heart (1 Samuel 1:12–15) and she got the answer to her prayer. From her example, you can see that a prayer posture is not so much a physical position. No wonder the Amplified Bible uses the words “heartfelt” and “persistent” to describe prayer in James 5:16. This is the kind of prayer that amounts to something.

As a bonus: even while you are praying, you can be distracted. However, you can win the battle of focus by patiently persevering by the help of the Holy Spirit.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
And you will seek Me and find Me, when you search for Me with all your heart.
(Jeremiah 29:13)
🙏 PRAYER
Father, thank You for the privilege to seek after You. I ask that You teach me patience while calling upon You. Also, rebuke every distraction, in the name of Jesus.
