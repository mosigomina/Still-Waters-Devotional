OCTOBER 1

Get Life from the Spirit

And the Lord God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living being. (Genesis 2:7)

In the beginning, God made His first big move on humankind. First, He made a shell for man out of the dust of the ground, which was the foundation. Then He breathed into this dust the breath of life, and man became a living being.

Much later, Apostle Paul uses this in his argument to remind the Corinthian church of what a human being is once everything is taken away—a thing that God has given life (1 Corinthians 15:45).

It’s easy to forget this idea: that even the greatest Christian alive today, walking in righteousness, smashing to pieces the works of Satan, has no life in or by themselves. Yes, it is a new life. The Bible even teaches us that because you believe in the Son of God, you have eternal life (1 John 5:10–11). But this is still the life you have received from God.

Whether it is the foundation for this new life or advancement by way of spiritual growth, it is still something you receive from God.

Do you want more life, more of the life that came because of Christ Jesus dying on the cross and rising from the grave? Look to God with all your heart and mind today, so you can live.

Yes, Bible study can do much, and prayer can do a lot. But these are only beneficial because God is their core and focus. The Lord Jesus puts it so beautifully in John 6:63—it is the Spirit who gives life; the flesh profits nothing.

It is not prayer that gives the life. It is not Bible study. It is not conviction. It is not even faith. None of these things are automatic magnets for life or more life. It is God who gives the life. He is the true origin, the true commander and conductor of the life.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
And the Lord God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living being.
(Genesis 2:7)
🙏 PRAYER
Heavenly Father, thank You for the many things You give. I acknowledge that You are the true source of the life. Please, teach me how to live in it more fully and give me grace to live it out.
