OCTOBER 28

Transformation through Renewal

And do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, that you may prove what is that good and acceptable and perfect will of God. (Romans 12:2)

Have you ever wondered why people do certain things, things that genuinely make you feel awe simply because of the sheer beauty or audacity of these things? That’s what happens when we put our minds to work.

The journey of transformation in the life of a believer in Christ begins with their minds. Even as a human being, it is possible for me to think things through in my mind and afterward project them physically. Why—because I have fully processed these things in my mind.

On your journey to spiritual maturity, one highly regarded requirement is a constant and continuous renewal of your mind through the Word of God. The Word of God is the tool, the perfect tool, that you can use to aid your growth and knowledge of Christ.

Renewal of the mind through the Word happens when you follow the instructions and corrections that come to make you conform to righteousness. How well you behold the Word of God is how well your mind will be transformed. At the end, this is what births spiritual growth.

As a sidenote, you can know if you are truly beholding the Word of God. It is all a matter of whether the Word has enough influence over you to make you willing to obey it. Therefore, when your mind is being renewed and transformed by the Word of God, quite a number of things in your life will be probed and prodded, and, if they are not consistent with God’s ways as revealed in Scripture, eventually taken out.

To get your mind renewed, there are at least three things you can do. The first is to study the Word of God daily. The second is to meditate on the Word of God that you studied. This means, sitting down with it to get God’s perspective on matters, not to argue with the text. The third is a daily confession of the Word in areas of your life where the help of God appears scarce.

Renewing your mind is an absolute requirement as a believer. Without it, you won’t be qualified or able to test and approve that which is God’s will—His good, pleasing and perfect will.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
And have put on the new man who is renewed in knowledge according to the image of Him who created him.
(Colossians 3:10)
🙏 PRAYER
Father of truth, guide me to pay even closer attention to the things of my mind, and continually work towards its renewal and transformation.
