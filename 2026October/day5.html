OCTOBER 5

Conviction by the Word

Be diligent to present yourself approved to God, a worker who does not need to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth. (2 Timothy 2:15)

Children are often attracted to cartoons because of the vibrant colours, humorous element, and simplified storytelling. Children are also easily convinced. They believe that literally anything is possible—that a vegetable without legs can walk! This is why it is very important to feed them from a tender age with the testimonies of Jesus, with His miracles and promises. But all of this is by the way.

As soon as the reality of the world sets in, whether in form of pain, sadness, wickedness, greed, needs, anxiety, pride, and more, a person who is not actively renewing his mind will unknowingly take up (and tightly embrace) a sinister conviction like ‘eat or be eaten.’

Someone once reported a teenager who said he wants to be wicked because, based on his observation, wicked people live longer than good people. Meanwhile, the Bible doesn’t say that wickedness brings long life. No, it associates long life with setting your love upon God and calling on Him (Psalm 91:14–16), as well as honour for fathers and mothers, whether biological or spiritual (Exodus 20:12).

This is why your convictions must be fixated on God’s Word, not your experiences or those of others.

It is an abomination when a commandment, an instruction, or a promise of God is quoted or taken out of context from Scripture (which is the inspired Word of God), then a believer in Christ says “let us face reality.” No! The Word is your reality. It may not be apparent to outsiders. But to you, child of God, it must be clear. Human beliefs do not make God unfaithful. Let God be true and every man a liar (Romans 3:3–4).

To have conviction is to hold your beliefs firmly, being very persuaded of them. You only achieve this by paying attention to the Word of God. Otherwise, your beliefs will never become convictions; your convictions will never become pillars for sound decisions; and your decisions will never please God.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Be diligent to present yourself approved to God, a worker who does not need to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.
(2 Timothy 2:15)
🙏 PRAYER
Dear Father, I have put my trust in You and Your Word. Help me build a heart of faith that is strong and steadfast, always trusting in You.
