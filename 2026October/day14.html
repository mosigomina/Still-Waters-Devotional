OCTOBER 14

Let God Convince You

Now the serpent was more cunning than any beast of the field which the Lord God had made. And he said to the woman, “Has God indeed said, ‘You shall not eat of every tree of the garden’?” (Genesis 3:1)

God has a clear expectation for how each generation is to follow Him correctly. In Genesis 18:19, He says about Abraham—he will teach his children, and they will keep the way of the Lord. This directive appears simple, yet its implications are profound. For one, God pays attention to what you have learned and what you, in turn, are teaching.

Consider the striking example of the Rekabite family in Jeremiah 35. God challenged Jeremiah to contrast this family with the unfaithful people of Judah and Jerusalem. In verse 14 (NIV), He notes: “Jehonadab son of Rekab ordered his descendants not to drink wine and this command has been kept. To this day they do not drink wine, because they obey their forefather’s command.”

For the Rekabites, their forefather’s instruction clearly transformed them at a fundamental level. Their unwavering obedience stemmed not from mere knowledge or intellectual understanding, but from a deep-seated conviction that their forefather’s teaching was profoundly worth following.

This illustrates the true nature of following God and cultivating spiritual growth. God will not frown at you for honestly examining His truth, what He has taught, instructed, or commanded. What He is opposed to is knowing His truth and then deliberately choosing to disregard it.

Furthermore, this isn’t only about big and measurable acts of disobedience, like outright refusal to obey clear biblical commands. It also manifests in the ‘small things’: how seriously you prepare yourself to receive teaching and instruction; the ease with which you forgive those who wrong you; the steadfastness of your love for fellow believers; and countless other daily choices. Usually, it is in these seemingly minor actions that God tests the depth and quality of your conviction.

Open yourself up to learning so that God can persuade you. Until you are fully convinced of God’s truths (of what He has revealed through His Word and the experiences of others about Himself and His ways), you have no assurance of being able to stand in the day of temptation and adversity.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
But you must continue in the things which you have learned and been assured of, knowing from whom you have learned them.
(2 Timothy 3:14)
🙏 PRAYER
God of light and truth, Your teaching contains all the revelation I need. Help me be diligent in searching out, deeply internalising, and firmly holding onto the truths You have provided in Your Word and through the experiences of others.
