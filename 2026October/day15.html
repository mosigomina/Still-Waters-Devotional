OCTOBER 15

Sow Life, Reap Life

For those who live according to the flesh set their minds on the things of the flesh, but those who live according to the Spirit, the things of the Spirit. (Romans 8:5)

A law is a concept that helps us understand how things work, an idea that helps you identify the factors and forces operating within a context, along with their purposes and extents. There are many such laws in the visible world, from gravity to thermodynamics. Some of them even resemble or operate like spiritual laws, like the law of sowing and reaping.

In Romans 8:5, it is confirmed that you can determine what you sow in your spiritual experience: either carnality (setting your mind on the things of the flesh) or godliness (setting your mind on the things of the Spirit of God). Paul says in effect: if your mind is filled or stained with ungodly things, it is because you have been living like an ungodly person.

Usually, we invert the dynamic, thinking that living like an ungodly person is solely a result of setting your mind on ungodly things. The Bible, however, clarifies that this is not always the case; how you live can actually influence how you think.

No wonder the apostles of Jesus Christ gave clear directives on how you should live. James says, watch what you say (James 3:1–2). John says, go all out to love your brothers and sisters in Christ (1 John 4:7). Peter says, watch your conduct and go about like a holy and godly person (2 Peter 3:11). Paul says, be compassionate, kind, humble, gentle, and patient (Colossians 3:12).

There are many such instructions in the Bible, and now you know why they are there. If you practice them, you can directly improve the quality of your spiritual life and experience. If you sow attention to the Word of God, you will reap both understanding (Proverbs 4:1) and progress (1 Timothy 4:15).

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Meditate on these things; give yourself entirely to them, that your progress may be evident to all.
(1 Timothy 4:15)
🙏 PRAYER
Heavenly Father, thank You for the law of sowing and reaping. Today, grant me wisdom and grace to sow seeds of eternal life, so that I can make progress in my devotion to You.
