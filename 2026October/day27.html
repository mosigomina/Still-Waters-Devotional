OCTOBER 27

The Mind of Christ

Let this mind be in you which was also in Christ Jesus… (Philippians 2:5)

The mind is a very powerful instrument. So powerful is it that everything seems to have started from it. For the believer in Christ Jesus, having your mind renewed is the same as having the edges of your spirit sharpened. No longer would you need to exert human strength and will to the point of being frustrated. Instead, by properly guiding your renewed mind by God’s Word and Spirit, spiritual growth and progress will come naturally.

Everything begins from the mind; the book of Genesis is clear on this. The mind was the precursor of creation, setting down a thought. Everything that you can see now was first preconceived in the mind of God. They were born in God’s mind before they ever came into tangible, visible, measurable manifestation.

Your spiritual growth works the same way. It is first preconceived in your mind before it ever comes into full manifestation. Therefore, if there is going to be spiritual advancement, it would first be conceived in your mind. And if there is going to be spiritual regression, it too will first be conceived in your mind.

To grow in the spirit, you need a focused and purpose-driven mind. The probability that you will experience any kind of spiritual growth with a distracted and perverted mind is absolutely, exactly, unquestionably zero.

After the salvation that God has accomplished in your life through Christ, the next best goal is the renewal of your mind. Only with a renewed mind can there be a transformation at the roots, as promised in Romans 12:2. As the Bible verse goes on to say, then, you would know and taste the will of God—which is good and acceptable and perfect (NASB).

So, yes, the key to pleasing the Father is conformity to the image of His Son (Romans 8:29). The key to conformity to the image of the Son is the renewal of your mind. And the key to the renewal of your mind is paying attention (with all seriousness) to the promptings and consecrations of the Lord, through the Scripture and through His Spirit.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
And do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, that you may prove what is that good and acceptable and perfect will of God.
(Romans 12:2)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, everything I am and have, including my mind, is Yours. Grant me the soberness, wisdom, patience, and grace I need to renew this mind for Your glory.
