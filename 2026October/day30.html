OCTOBER 30

Overcoming Sin and Temptation

I have written to you, fathers, because you have known Him who is from the beginning. I have written to you, young men, because you are strong, and the Word of God abides in you, and you have overcome the wicked one. (1 John 2:14)

The Bible is clear on the link between spiritual progress and growth and temptation. It does not say that with growth, the dispensation of temptation is past. It does not say that because of growth, you are now allowed to fall to every temptation. What it says is that you are growing when you can rise above a temptation and come out victorious (1 Corinthians 10:13).

This is an interesting link. Going further, the Bible seems to say that this victory comes because faith has been built up in your heart (1 John 5:4). It also ties to this equation the fact that the building of faith comes through the Word of God and has the effect of your convictions becoming solidified and your progress becoming obvious (1 Timothy 4:15).

According to the Scripture, God has already made His intentions about temptations clear: He will not tempt you more than you can bear (1 Corinthians 10:13). In fact, the verse goes on to reveal that God has prepared a way of escape so that you can outlive the temptation.

The meaning of this is profound: that God has already provided every resource you need to successfully go through challenges; that He knows your capacity and had that in His mind before the temptation came; that, through the temptation, you can become even more victorious over sin.

Being able to continually overcome sin, besetting or otherwise, is a sign that you are growing in faith. It is a sign that shows that your strength in God is also growing. After all, the Bible says in effect in Proverbs 24:10: if your strength fails in days of adversity, it only means that your strength is small or not sufficient enough.

The strength that has been built over time via the Word of God is what will cause you to rise above sin and temptation when you are faced with it. So, build strength in the Word of God. It is what you have meditated on over time that He (the Spirit of Truth) will bring to your remembrance, especially at every arrival of the sin that lurks around your heart and is bent on making you fall.

The judgments of the Lord are true; they are righteous altogether (Psalm 19:9b). If you don’t want to keep yielding to temptation and sin, yield to the Word of God and stay with it.
