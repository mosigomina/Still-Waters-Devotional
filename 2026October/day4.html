OCTOBER 4

Brokenness and Humility

The Lord is near to those who have a broken heart, and saves such as have a contrite spirit. (Psalm 34:18)

When brokenness comes to mind, the tendency is to think of a person with a sad expression. Yes, true brokenness involves some level of soberness or solemnity, but it is more about inward contemplation. For a believer pressing on towards perfection, brokenness and humility are absolute necessities.

Brokenness stems from a deeper work of conviction by the Holy Spirit. It is the process by which God chips away at your self-sufficiency and pride, revealing your inherent weaknesses. Sometimes, He even does this in the midst of conditions that are able to bring you to your knees. In this process of being brought to your knees, true spiritual growth can occur.

Mournful contemplation leads you to the point of yielding yourself to the working of the Lord in your life. This is a journey well seen in Psalm 51.

In the first five verses, you can see a man contemplate himself before God. From verse six, you see the same man. But this time, having contemplated, he demonstrates readiness to be worked upon. And a truly broken man is a humble man.

This is the man God lifts up and closer to Himself. This is the kind of person that God deems fit, the kind of person He happily gives strength to and causes to walk steadily with grace and power (Isaiah 40:28–31).

It is important to hold this dearly: brokenness and humility are necessities. When you truly mourn, you qualify for God’s comforting. When you do not, divine comfort will not come. And where there is no true brokenness, there is no true surrender. Only with a surrendered heart can you grow consistently in God.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
The Lord is near to those who have a broken heart, and saves such as have a contrite spirit.
(Psalm 34:18)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, help me to humble myself before you enough to be worked upon.
