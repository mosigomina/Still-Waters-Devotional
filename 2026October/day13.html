OCTOBER 13

Shed Some Weight

Therefore, having these promises, beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from all filthiness of the flesh and spirit, perfecting holiness in the fear of God. (2 Corinthians 7:1)

God is utterly honest. When He calls us to Himself, He offers no illusions. He makes it clear that the road ahead is bumpy, and that it is a path that can only be trodden by His grace. He warns us of things that can hinder our journey to His desired destination. In His matchless wisdom, He calls these hindrances ‘weights.’

Throughout Scripture, we see these ‘weights’ consistently present in the lives of those who walked with God. For some, they were clear-cut sins—actions God explicitly commanded His people to detach from (1 Corinthians 10:6). For others, these weights were legitimate privileges (Hebrews 11:24–26), even necessary responsibilities (Matthew 8:21–22) or daily concerns (John 6:27). The common thread is anything that hinders spiritual progress.

It is indeed profound wisdom to pay attention to these weights. God’s perspective is clear: not only are they undesirable because they can hinder your growth, but you must actively cast them far away from you, for they can even kill your spiritual life.

If you are truly committed to the path of spiritual growth, you cannot be escorted by the flesh. The road to life is narrow precisely because it only has space for what aligns with God. And the ‘flesh,’ that aspect of your nature that is inherently opposed to God, has no place on that path.

Therefore, the Scripture commands us to cleanse ourselves from everything that can defile us, perfecting holiness to demonstrate to God that we fear Him (2 Corinthians 7:1). This means shedding every weight: from the obvious burdens of worry, anxiety, and sin, to all other enticements the flesh offers.

These weights, particularly sin, can set you back years in an instant. The only way to successfully run the race God has set before you and finish strong is to diligently lay aside every single one.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Therefore we also, since we are surrounded by so great a cloud of witnesses, let us lay aside every weight, and the sin which so easily ensnares us, and let us run with endurance the race that is set before us…
(Hebrews 12:1)
🙏 PRAYER
O God, my Deliverer, this world is indeed a place laden with weights. Yet, I desire only Your light yoke upon my heart. Please, deliver me from every other weight that hinders my walk with You.
