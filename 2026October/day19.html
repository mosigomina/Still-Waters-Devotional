OCTOBER 19

Pursuing Spiritual Maturity

But solid food belongs to those who are of full age, that is, those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil. (Hebrews 5:14)

God has a plan for your life: it is that you grow into maturity. It is that under His careful cultivation, you would digest the nourishment He has supplied into your spirit through His Spirit. Then, your spiritual growth will become measurable, your heart surrendered, your will submitted, and a future with God guaranteed.

1 Peter 2:2 declares God’s intentions: that, like a newborn baby, crave [thirst for and earnestly desire] the pure milk of the Word of God, so that you “may be nurtured and grow unto [completed] salvation…” (Amplified Bible, Classic Edition). In other words, don’t remain a baby. Grow up in all things into Christ.

In God’s eyes, it’s possible that you are a baby. Even though being a spiritual infant is a natural starting point, it’s not meant to be a permanent state. When children are still at the early stage of infancy, they desire breast milk. However, after a while, they transition to other liquids like pap and custard. After a while, they are once again encouraged to change what they eat.

For the natural child, it’s a progressive journey of growth. For you, O child of God, it is also a progressive journey, especially because you are also a disciple of the Lord Jesus Christ. God, being wise, put that desire for food in the natural child so they will not starve, remain weak, and die. The same God has made His Word available to you so you can grow from strength to strength and in grace.

But here’s another important point: unlike the natural child, God will not force His Word down your throat. To submit to the desire and pursuit of spiritual maturity, He leaves the decision to you. It is a decision that you must take up with sincerity of heart. After all, pursuing spiritual maturity is a lifelong journey; you cannot do it right without serious and intentional efforts, serious and intentional dedication, and a deep relationship with God, also serious and intentional.

By cultivating a personal relationship with God through prayers, worship, and continued study of His Word, spiritual maturity will come. By constant faith, obedience, and fruitfulness, you will enjoy a progressive transformation from spiritual infancy to maturity. And as you continue, you will grow in discernment and wisdom on how to navigate life’s journey and challenges, and make godly decisions.

Dear believer, spiritual maturity cannot be overemphasised. You cannot afford to be a half-baked Christian on this journey, so, don’t be one. Be all that God wants you to be.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
…as newborn babes, desire the pure milk of the word, that you may grow thereby...
(1 Peter 2:2)
🙏 PRAYER
