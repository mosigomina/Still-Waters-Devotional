OCTOBER 29

Choose This Day!

And if it seems evil to you to serve the Lord, choose for yourselves this day whom you will serve, whether the gods which your fathers served that were on the other side of the River, or the gods of the Amorites, in whose land you dwell. But as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord.” And the people said to Joshua, “No, but we will serve the Lord!” “Now therefore,” he said, “put away the foreign gods which are among you, and incline your heart to the Lord God of Israel.” And the people said to Joshua, “The Lord our God we will serve, and His voice we will obey! (Joshua 24:15, 21, 23–24)

One of the greatest determinants of the quality of life, Christ-like life, that a believer can have is choice. Good choices make up a good quality of life. Bad choices characterise a bad quality of life. The Lord, wise and deliberate, has things He wants accomplished in such a believer’s life. But it is up to the believer to conform to the ways of the Lord or their own.

This is why the foremost choice of a man is to choose whether or not to be separated unto God. Deciding to be separated to God is to choose to renounce every other thing that can (and has) become an idol to you. Here is a simple test: is there anything that you are attached to more than you are attached to Jesus? That thing is your idol.

In the part of Abrahams’s story where God demanded the sacrifice of Isaac, something significant was said: “Take your son, your only son Isaac, whom you love…” The words ‘only’ and ‘love’ there show the weight of Isaac to Abraham.

But Abraham proved that even though there was a weight of significance that Isaac had in his life, God was his God. Not Isaac. If God wanted to do away with what He gave, that is, Isaac, to Abraham, that was fine. To deny God for Isaac was not worth it. It was not comparable to the lordship of God in his life.

No wonder! In the Scripture, God told the Israelites to consecrate themselves because He was the Lord their God. This is where the Lord wants us to be. Because He is our God, He wants us to be willing and able to forsake all attachments and put away all gods in our hearts.
