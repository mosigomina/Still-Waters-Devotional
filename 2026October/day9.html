OCTOBER 9

How Balanced is Your Diet?

But we will give ourselves continually to prayer and to the ministry of the Word. (Acts 6:4)

For a healthy, balanced spiritual life, a believer needs two fundamental ingredients. These aren’t mere suggestions; the Scripture consistently reveals them as constants in the lives of Jesus Christ and His disciples. These vital ingredients are prayer and the study of God’s Word.

Spiritual deficiency can manifest in several ways: having neither a consistent prayer life nor a firm study life; neglecting prayer while prioritising Bible study; giving precedence to prayer but neglecting deliberate and mindful study of God’s Word.

Our featured Scripture today highlights how the disciples devoted themselves primarily to prayer and the study of God’s Word. The Bible consistently records Jesus’ teaching for all His followers (including you) to pray using the revealed Word of God (which we know today as the Bible), to live a life of consistent communication with the Father, and to diligently study the sacred texts. This was His own pattern of life.

In God’s spiritual cookbook, only a balanced recipe guarantees a balanced spiritual life. Only when prayer and the Word are combined in their prescribed measures can true, balanced growth occur. You simply cannot overemphasise one, especially given the imperative to grow into your destiny of conformity with Christ.

If you elevate one over the other or treat either as unimportant, your spiritual growth will inevitably be slanted. This means certain areas of your life will strongly contradict the balanced pattern of growth stipulated in the Bible.

In conclusion, it’s crucial to understand that a healthy prayer life is rooted in the written Word of God, and a healthy study life propels you to communicate with God more effectively.
