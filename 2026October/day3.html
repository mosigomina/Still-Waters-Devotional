OCTOBER 3

Meditating on God’s Word

Oh, how I love Your law! It is my meditation all the day. You, through Your commandments, make me wiser than my enemies; for they are ever with me. I have more understanding than all my teachers, for Your testimonies are my meditation. Through Your precepts I get understanding; therefore I hate every false way. (Psalm 119:97–99, 104)

Meditation can be defined as the art of musing over or reflecting upon. As humans who possess the ability to think, meditation is unavoidable.

In Philippians 4, the Apostle Paul gives the members of the church in Philippi instructions on how to conduct themselves as believers. In verse eight, Paul instructs: ”Meditate!”

This isn’t the first time meditation is set forward as an instruction, as a must. In the popular Joshua 1:8, God wasn’t suggesting to Joshua to meditate. No, He was giving the condition for Joshua’s life progress: “...but you shall meditate…”

These divine instructions to meditate have a focus: the inspired words of God. The Word of God is where you can find an ultimate compilation of everything that is noble, right, pure, praiseworthy and of virtue. Sadly, meditation is not frequently spoken of or considered in the same level as other components for growing spiritually.

In 1 Timothy 4:15, Apostle Paul writes to his son Timothy and instructs him to meditate on the things that have been said to him and to give himself entirely to them. Why—so that his progress would be evident to all. Paul didn’t just tell him to obey; he also told him to meditate. It wasn’t one or the other, but one AND the other—and this was absolutely necessary for his progress.

Reading through Psalm 119, David attributes how he understands the law of God to how he had meditated upon them. This shows that a lack of understanding and an inclination to wrong paths can be symptoms of not meditating on the Word of God (verse 104).

God has not hidden from you the secret of what made men like David, Paul, Timothy, and Joshua. One of them is meditation.

If you’ve gotten this far without meditation, think how much further you can go with meditation. Begin to take steps towards it: one Bible verse at a time, one chapter at a time, and see the Lord helping you.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
I have more understanding than all my teachers, for Your testimonies are my meditation.
(Psalm 119:99)
🙏 PRAYER
Father, guide my heart into meditating on Your Word. Help me lay aside every distraction in favour of it.
