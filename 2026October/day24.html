OCTOBER 24

Spiritual Fruit and Character

But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control. Against such there is no law. (Galatians 5:22–23)

Bearing spiritual fruit and character is similar to seed planting. When a farmer plants a seed, he doesn’t immediately expect to see the result of his labour. He waits a while, using what he knows of the kind of seed planted to determine how long he needs to wait. It is the same with the Word of God in you. The way to have it bear fruit is to let it go through all of its necessary processes, even as you go through all of your necessary processing by it.

The same way the farmer has expectations of the seed, God has expectations of you. He expects that after investing so much of His word in you, you will bear fruits according to the same word. In other words, the fruits God is expecting are those that please Him, the type spoken of in Galatians 5:22–23.

These fruits have the ability to affect your character, on the condition that you are a believer that is determined to grow spiritually. It is on the path of fruit-bearing that even a stranger would see you and, by your disposition and character, begin to recognise that you are genuinely born again.

Dear believer, your walk with God should affect your speech, conduct, and appearances. The transformation that the Spirit of God in you guarantees is bound to affect these things, which is why God is saying to you now, “Be an example to all believers in what you say, in the way you live, in your love, your faith, and your purity” (1 Timothy 4:12).

A little extra to ponder: if the spiritual growth you are attaining has not affected (and does not seem like it will ever affect) your character, that growth needs to be checked. The Bible is clear on this: “by their fruits you shall know them” (Matthew 7:16,20). How you identify someone who is growing spiritually is by their fruit and character. It is not by their principles—it is by their fruit and character.

Dear believer, it is possible for you to just be a tree without fruits. But what God wants is that you grow and bear fruits of love, fruits of patience, fruits of longsuffering, and more.

(2 Peter 1:5–7)
🙏 PRAYER
