OCTOBER 6

Present in His Presence

One thing I have desired of the Lord, that will I seek: that I may dwell in the house of the Lord all the days of my life, to behold the beauty of the Lord, and to inquire in His temple. (Psalm 27:4)

A good number of people who were born into Christian homes have—or at least had—the culture of morning devotion. A few out of this number also had night prayer and devotion before bedtime with the help of devotionals like this one. Memory verses were dutifully recited, Bible passages were read and a simplified explanation was given. But most importantly, meeting with God daily was a constant, a must.

However, there usually is also a great deal of forgetfulness. Immediately the book is closed, everyone goes their way, thinking that they have given to God the 30 minutes allocated to Him for the day. You might not even recall the previous topic or verse, let alone put to practice what you had learnt, as Joshua 1:8 commands.

The charge today is not to make you despise or look down on morning devotion. The charge is to remind you to desire to be like the Psalmist: to seek the Lord to the point of dwelling in His presence all day long—and even for the rest of your life (Psalm 27:4).

The desire for God’s presence births meditation on the person of Jesus Christ. It also guarantees a controlled mental climate. It is the same way you don’t have to tell people who are in love to think about each other all day. As you set your love on Jesus and put Him before your eyes all day long, your devotion to Him will get stronger.

The Bible guarantees that when you turn to the Lord (and keep your gaze trained on Him), the veil is taken away. Then, you will begin to look like, sound like, and think like Him, even in the heat of emotional turbulence. Then it will be true that you know the One you are worshipping and walking with (John 4:21–22).

📖 MEMORY VERSE
One thing I have desired of the Lord, that will I seek: that I may dwell in the house of the Lord all the days of my life, to behold the beauty of the Lord, and to inquire in His temple.
(Psalm 27:4)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord Jesus, this world is a busy and turbulent place. I want to be present with You in my heart and mind. Please, help me.
