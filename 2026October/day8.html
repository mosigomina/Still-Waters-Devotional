OCTOBER 8

Don’t Cut the Roots Off!

For in Him we live and move and have our being, as also some of your own poets have said, ‘For we are also His offspring.’ (Acts 17:28)

God is the source and foundation of all existence. That includes everything from the acacia trees in the Sahara to the polar bears in Greenland, the dragonfish in the depths of the sea, you, me. If it lives, if it moves, if it dies, God is its ultimate origin.

The Scripture teaches from Apostle Paul’s short sermon at the Council of the Areopagus (Acts 17:22–31) that you are God’s offspring. More than that, in Him you live and move and have your being. So, you have the trademark of life because the living God made you. As Apostle John said: All things were made through Him, and without Him nothing was made that was made (John 1:3).

A significant problem today is that many are trying to live, move, and exist outside of God. It’s the classic scenario of a fish trying to live out of water, or a tree with no interest in soil—it simply cannot work. This is why even devout people of faith can experience burnout or stop growing. If you don’t situate yourself in God and anchor your roots to Him, weariness and stagnation are inevitable.

Have you ever seen a tree floating disconnected from the ground and still thriving? Not in this world. A tree severed from its roots, no longer tied to the earth, will inevitably stop growing. And where there is no growth, there is only death.

Our message today is simple: if you deliberately or unintentionally uproot yourself from God, your source and foundation, it won’t be long before you stop growing. This isn’t just about your faith; it applies to your consecration and the transformative power of God’s life within you. And if you stop growing—if those roots are severed—death will surely come.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
All things were made through Him, and without Him nothing was made that was made.
(John 1:3)
🙏 PRAYER
Dear God, You have shown me kindness by reminding me of the need to stay rooted. Let my roots grow downward into Christ, as I become more and more like Him.
