OCTOBER 21

The Joint Supplies

…from whom the whole body, joined and knit together by what every joint supplies, according to the effective working by which every part does its share, causes growth of the body for the edifying of itself in love. (Ephesians 4:6)

The dispensation we currently live in is one where people normally pride themselves on being self-sufficient and self-involved (that is, absorbed in their own interests or thoughts). Of course, this is only illusory since God did not create any one of us to be self-sufficient. Sadly, even believers in Christ fail to realise that we (disciples of the Lord Jesus) need one another to grow.

When a person becomes born-again, God ushers them into His family. In that family, every member is tasked with supplying something for the growth and functionality of the others.

This is the idea behind the body of Christ. 1 Corinthians 12:12–26 explains it thoroughly. In fact, we are informed that “If one part suffers, every part suffers with it; if one part is honoured, every part rejoices with it.”

Do you know that God gave us the spiritual gifts recorded in 1 Corinthians 12:4–11 for one purpose and one purpose only? The Easy English Bible translation makes verse 7 of that passage very plain: “God helps each of us with a gift from his Spirit. He does that so that we can help others.” The Living Bible (TLB) is more direct: “The Holy Spirit displays God’s power through each of us as a means of helping the entire church.”

The essence of the different Bible translations is the same: God gives these gifts so that all the members of the body of Christ will benefit. This body of Christ in question is not a catchphrase; it is a real thing. It has a head, which is Christ Jesus. Being joined to Christ in His death, burial, and resurrection (Romans 6:3–4; Ephesians 2:4–6), God has granted us that identity as members of His body.

Considering all these, how can you be self-sufficient or refuse to participate in the natural functions of this body? Once again, for a natural human body, the head needs the neck, the tongue needs the mouth, the fingers need the hands, and everything relies on the directions from the head. So it is for the body of Christ: we are jointly fitted together to help each other grow.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
For as we have many members in one body, but all the members do not have the same function, so we, being many, are one body in Christ, and individually members of one another.
(Romans 12:4–5)
🙏 PRAYER
Heavenly Father, thank You for bringing me into Your family and gifting me brothers and sisters after Your heart. Please, help me be selfless enough to open myself to them and walk with them in all that pleases You.
