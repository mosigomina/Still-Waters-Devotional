OCTOBER 22

Community and Fellowship

Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as is the manner of some, but exhorting one another, and so much the more as you see the Day approaching. (Hebrews 10:25)

The Lord God has given His people many gifts. He has given the gift of salvation, which is through the Lord Jesus Christ. He has also given the person of the Holy Spirit. There is another gift that God has graciously given to His children: the gathering and community of brethren (also known as the believers in Christ Jesus).

In the Scripture, you can see a track record of believers always dwelling together. This is the norm by God’s standards, so that the brethren (brothers and sisters in Christ) are always encouraging and exalting one another, even with songs and hymns.

To despise the gathering of brethren is to despise the gift of God. To think that you are superior to this gathering is to think that you are superior to Christ Jesus, the Head of the body of brethren (Ephesians 5:29–30). Despising the gathering of believers will do you no good.

A sign of spiritual growth is that you love the fellowship (the communion, companionship, and harmony) of the brethren. God sets out many instructions for this purpose: for example, Romans 12:10. As you practice these instructions, God marks you as someone interested in—and making—spiritual progress.

God has not destined you to run this race entirely alone. You need a community of people who share the same mind and passion as you. Such a community can serve as a check against excesses in your walk with God. After all, God has saddled the body with the particular duty of helping you grow, even as He grants grace (Ephesians 4:11–13).

God has given the brethren the responsibility to continue the Apostles’ doctrine. Therefore, being a member of the body of Christ, you must be accountable to members of your local assembly. That is where God has prepared for you to be discipled, so that you can reach the heights intended for you.

🙏 PRAYER
