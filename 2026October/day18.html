OCTOBER 18

Spiritual Growth through Faith in Christ Jesus

But grow in grace, and in the knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ. To Him be glory both now and for ever. Amen. (2 Peter 3:18)

You can tell that a believer in Christ Jesus will continue to experience spiritual growth by the quality of their faith. That is, you can say with almost perfect certainty that such and such a person will remain spiritually stagnant because their faith is small, stunted, not straightforward, or scattered rather than focused.

In God’s great blueprint, spiritual growth happens along the pathway of faith in Christ Jesus. According to 2 Peter 3:18, this is the first part of the equation. Receiving Jesus Christ happened because of faith—as Romans 10:10 states, in your heart was belief unto righteousness, and in the mouth confession unto salvation (Berean Literal Bible). So also, growing in your relationship with God comes about through faith.

What are we saying? That your growth as a believer and disciple of the Lord Jesus is tied to your level of faith and trust in God. Even if you ignore the complex dynamics of this truth, surely you know that to believe that God will do something you have never seen before, you need faith. In the same way, to grow in your conformity with Christ (which is the shared destiny of every one God has called), you need faith in God’s will and in His process of transformation inside you.

The second part of Apostle Peter’s equation of spiritual growth is knowledge, specifically, knowledge of the Lord Jesus Christ. The way Peter phrased it, there is no way you can believe and go on believing in the Lord Jesus Christ without knowing Him in an increasing measure.

For this endeavour, you can read Peter’s instruction as, “grow in grace, so you can increase in your knowledge (and daily experience) of the Lord Jesus Christ, so you can grow in more grace to know Him even more. Such a wonderful dynamic! But, as Romans 10:17 makes clear, both knowledge of the Saviour Jesus Christ and faith in Him only come from hearing the Word of God.

So, spiritual growth through faith in the Lord Jesus is absolutely a transformative journey. It involves fully and consciously trusting the Lord and even growing in this trust. It also involves a commitment to your relationship with God, to spiritual discipline, and to obedience. These are all signs of spiritual growth as determined by God, and they are only possible when you build and solidify faith by the Word of God.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
“The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart” (that is, the word of faith which we preach).
(Romans 10:8b)
🙏 PRAYER
