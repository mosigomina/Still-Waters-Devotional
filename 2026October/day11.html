OCTOBER 11

Participate in the Process

As you therefore have received Christ Jesus the Lord, so walk in Him, rooted and built up in Him and established in the faith, as you have been taught, abounding in it with thanksgiving. (Colossians 2:6–7)

It is possible to live like a Christian on autopilot. All you would need to do is disengage or rely on the energy of others. Among Christians, there are those who can pray intensely, but only when others are ‘fired up’. They will not lift their hands or close their eyes because the song is not stirring their hearts or sending them goose pimples. This passive approach, where your engagement depends entirely on external cues, is a big problem.

And this is the passive believer at a glance: their spiritual tempo is dictated by external circumstances like trouble or excitement, rather than their own initiative. This passivity leads to a disconnect: walking with God without fully participating in the process. It’s like praying words without even paying attention to what you are saying, or hearing God’s Word taught without being on the lookout for how it applies to your present circumstances or how you can make adjustments because of it.

When Apostle Paul urges us to ‘walk in Christ Jesus as we have received Him’ (Colossians 2:6–7), he’s calling for personal responsibility. He means going beyond merely ‘getting your legs wet’ in spiritual matters, or only speaking in tongues when at church or feeling emotionally charged.

Participate fully in the process! Pour yourself into the transformative work God is doing within you and through you. This means rejecting two extremes: neither passively saying, “He’ll take the wheel, and things will just play out,” nor remaining dull and disengaged. Both paths allow sins and strongholds to persist in your life.

As a bonus: Hebrews 4:11 commands us to “be diligent to enter that rest,” which means putting serious effort into God’s promises. While Christ Jesus remains the vine and you are the branch, you, as the branch, must actively strive to remain in Him and bear fruit (John 15:5). At some point, God considers this progressive participation as faith itself. And without such active faith, it’s impossible to please God or experience spiritual growth.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Therefore, my beloved, as you have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling.
(Philippians 2:12)
🙏 PRAYER
Good Father, thank You for inviting me into Your transformative process. Forgive me for moments of passivity or disengagement. Please, empower me to fully participate with diligence and intention, trusting that my active faith truly pleases You.
