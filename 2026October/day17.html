OCTOBER 17

Count the Cost

For which of you, intending to build a tower, does not sit down first and count the cost, whether he has enough to finish it—lest, after he has laid the foundation, and is not able to finish, all who see it begin to mock him, saying, ‘This man began to build and was not able to finish’? (Luke 14:28–30)

For the Christian, discipleship is not a simple, extended mentorship programme. It has a focused goal with an eternal weight and worth: to become like Christ. However, becoming like Christ is not going to happen just like that. It requires many things, including an intentional consideration and action, namely, counting the cost.

Jesus does call people into a life of following Him. When He did that with the apostles in the Gospels, He was very clear with them about what it means and entails. And He placed the bar very high, as you can see from Luke 14:28–30.

Without a doubt, the cost of following Jesus Christ is just as high. It involves a forsaking—present continuous tense—of your life. Christ Himself made it clear that whoever wants to save their life will lose it (Matthew 16:25). In God’s kingdom, your life is something you lose.

This is a cost, one that seems like a disadvantage at first glance. However, the longer you consider it, the clearer you will see that the cost is a greater advantage. Really, this forsaking of your assured safety of life is what God considers your sharpest evidence of devotion to Him.

You should be familiar with the many individuals who, as recorded by the Bible and even in contemporary times, gave up earthly prestige and status, relationships, and their very lives for God. For the goal of attaining and becoming like Jesus, these people abandoned and renounced the pleasures of this world. These people should be your role models.

In all, the life that the Lord has called you into is one where you must consider daily and act intentionally. God is not hoping that you will put everything on the line to become like Jesus. No, God is expecting it! The cost for meeting this expectation will involve self-denial, but you must embrace this cost. This is sure to help you more deeply follow the Lord. Otherwise, you will only be building something now to abandon it later.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
…looking unto Jesus, the author and finisher of our faith, who for the joy that was set before Him endured the cross, despising the shame, and has sat down at the right hand of the throne of God.
(Hebrews 12:2)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, I praise You for Your wisdom and compassion in letting me know what to forsake and what to embrace. Please, I don’t only want to know the cost of becoming like Christ; I want to bear it. Grant me both wisdom and grace for this.
