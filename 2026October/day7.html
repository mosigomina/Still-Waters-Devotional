OCTOBER 7

A Spiritual Man

Whoever has been born of God does not sin, for His seed remains in him; and he cannot sin, because he has been born of God. (1 John 3:9)

In the school of purpose, self-discovery is a core course that you must take in order to advance or make any form of progress.

The reason our very intentional God puts us in families is so we can be named, nurtured, and instructed in values and ideals. This is the basis behind the common excuse that “We don’t do this where I’m from.”

You have been born (again) into the family of God. In this family, your Father is Spirit. If it is true (as stated in John 3:6) that whatever is born of spirit is spirit, you cannot be different from the other members of the family.

In this great family, true members do not sin (1 John 3:9).

Galatians 5:25 makes this point very clear: if you live in the spirit, you would walk in the spirit. If you walk in the spirit, you would not fulfill the desires of the flesh (Galatians 5:16). The works of the flesh and the fruit of the spirit are laid clearly in Galatians 5. Whoever has read this passage would be able to tell (when they come across you) who your Father is and whose child you really are.

The reason a child of God does not continue in sin is not that grace will bail him out. No! A child of God does not continue in sin because there is a surge of power to live above sin (Romans 6:1–2). This is more than self-discovery or awareness. It is “God discovery,” an awareness of the Spirit of God that every member of His family has been called to submit to.

For sure, God has given you all that is needed to arrive at that golden statement: “This is my beloved son in whom I am well-pleased!” Don’t let sin reign in your mortal body. Set yourself up to overcome by knowing who you are and, most importantly, whose you are.

📖 MEMORY VERSE

(1 John 3:9)
🙏 PRAYER
