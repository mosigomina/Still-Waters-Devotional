OCTOBER 31

Set God’s Pleasure Before You

And He who sent Me is with Me. The Father has not left Me alone, for I always do those things that please Him. (John 8:29)

Desire is a very strong thing. Much of what you will achieve today as a whole will rest on your desires. If you end up productive, it would be because you wanted to be productive. If you end up filled with the Holy Spirit, it is because you desired it. So, desire is strong enough to change the course of things. This is point one.

Point two is that because desires are this strong, God can be with them or against them. In other words, God can decide to rouse the hosts of Heaven on your behalf simply because of something you want, thought of, and are planning to act on today. And it can go either way, depending on what that thing is.

Hear what the Lord Jesus says in John 8:29—"And He who sent Me is with Me. The Father has not left Me alone, for I always do those things that please Him.” This means that when your desires align with God’s, you can experience His full partnership throughout today.

You can turn the statement the other way and be right to say that if you consistently do what displeases Him, you will almost certainly be disconnected from His presence and support.

To be sincere, it is a big deal to make pleasing God a daily pursuit. Forget the possibility of succeeding every time; it’s difficult to throw aside what pleases you, and instead, cultivate only what pleases God in your heart, mind, words, and actions.

It is not easy, but it is possible. The secret might lie in desire: to want to want it, and start working towards that want. It is what is presented to the brethren in 2 Corinthians 8:11–12—that there must first be a desire, which, when acted upon, can result in great things.

🙏 PRAYER
Lord God, here are the desires in my heart that I suspect are keeping me from being all that You want me to be. Here are others that are helping me still hear you. Please, do Your work in me. Help me root out these unpleasing desires. Help me strengthen the ones You approve.
