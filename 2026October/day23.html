OCTOBER 23

Loving a Community of Believers

By this we know love, because He laid down His life for us. And we also ought to lay down our lives for the brethren. (1 John 3:16)

The Bible gives many proofs for being a follower of Jesus Christ. One such proof is love. Not being a preacher of love, a singer of love, or a master of love. No—being a practitioner of love with your brothers and sisters in Christ. Or, to put it as the Lord Jesus puts it in John 13:34–35: “as I have loved you, you also, love one another.”

Love, in this context, is a commandment to do. And like every commandment from our Lord, your will is very much involved. Without your will, there’s no point. Only with your will can your response be defined as either obedience or disobedience to the commandment of the Lord Jesus.

You can decide either to love your brothers and sisters in Christ because there was a pinch in your heart or butterflies in your belly. Or you can decide to love them because God commanded it; and because you love God and will do whatever He says; and because the people He has commanded you to love are those over whom He has lavished Himself and His love and His grace.

Apostle Peter puts it so well in 1 Peter 2:17. He said, “Honor all people. Love the brotherhood. Fear God. Honor the king.” In other words, when it comes to the brethren, what you owe them, unceasingly, now and in the future, always, is love.

And this love is active. It is characterised by fondness, care, mutual interest, experiences, values, and, most importantly, a common destination called Eternity with God (Acts 4:32).

See, the world has a flawed view of love. Some people have researched it to no end and arrived at love languages (this is fine). But we know God is love and there is no greater love than what He has expressed: that He laid down His life for us, His friends, His children, and His brethren (John 15:13).

To prefer your brethren to yourself is not a natural thing. Humans are inherently selfish, after all. Therefore, you must intentionally practice love in your relationships. You must go out of your way to love your brothers and sisters in Christ. God, the Judge of all things, calls this the more excellent way (1 Corinthians 12:31). You cannot believe, think, or act otherwise.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Love suffers long and is kind; love does not envy; love does not parade itself, is not puffed up; does not behave rudely, does not seek its own, is not provoked, thinks no evil; does not rejoice in iniquity, but rejoices in the truth; bears all things, believes all things, hopes all things, endures all things. Love never fails. But whether there are prophecies, they will fail; whether there are tongues, they will cease; whether there is knowledge, it will vanish away.
(1 Corinthians 13:4–7)
🙏 PRAYER
Father of pure love, thank You for sharing Your truths with me today. Please, teach me how to love my brothers and sisters in Christ; to love them with all I am and all I have.
