July 20

You Are the Salt of the Earth

You are the salt of the earth; but if the salt loses its flavour, how shall it be seasoned? It is then good for nothing but to be thrown out and trampled underfoot by men. (Matthew 5:13)

As believers in Christ, God has called us salt because of our responsibility to influence the world positively. Salt preserves, purifies, and adds flavour. In the same way, Christians are called to preserve godly values, promote righteousness, and bring hope to a world that is increasingly drifting from God’s standards. A believer who lives according to God’s Word becomes a source of positive influence wherever he or she is found.

Our nation, Nigeria, faces serious challenges, including-but not limited to-corruption, injustice, moral decline, and division. Rather than blending into these problems, believers are called to be agents of change. Through honesty, integrity, compassion, and godly conduct, Christians can help preserve what is good and point others toward Christ. As Colossians 4:6 teaches, our words should be gracious and seasoned with salt, bringing wisdom, encouragement, and healing to those around us.

As a side note, the Lord Jesus warned that salt which loses its saltiness becomes useless (Luke 14:34-35). When believers compromise their faith and adopt the values of the world, they lose their effectiveness and influence.

What God desires is that His people remain distinct, maintaining their witness in every sphere of society. It is when we live faithfully and reflect Christ daily that we become the preserving and transforming influence that our communities and nation desperately need.

📖 Memory Verse

Ye are the salt of the earth: but if the salt have lost his savour, wherewith shall it be salted?

(Matthew 5:13)
🙏 Prayer

Father, help me to remain a faithful witness for Christ. Let my life, words, and actions preserve godly values and bring positive change to my community and Nigeria in Jesus’ name. Amen.
