July 11

All Authority Comes from God

You would have no power over me at all, unless it were given to you from above. (John 19:11)

The words of the Lord Jesus are often heavy and jaw-wrenching. Imagine what He says to Pontius Pilate in John 19:11, the man holding the legal power to crucify Him, in the very room where that decision was about to be made. It is one of the most striking moments of affirmation in all of Scripture. Standing before unjust judgment, facing a sentence He did not deserve, Jesus confirms Pilate’s authority and traces its source straight back to God.

This is a far harder example to sit with than the comfortable ways we usually talk about authority coming from God. Anybody can say the same thing about a ruler who is wise and fair. But imagine saying this about someone who, in our estimation, is cruel, selfish, uncaring.

But we have to take it as it is-as the Lord said it. Our consolation is that authority being from God comes paired with accountability for how it is used. Every exercise of it answers to the One who gave it.

This is the truth behind Romans 13:1, all authority appointed by God, and Proverbs 8:15-16, kings reigning and judges judging by Him. Power exercised wrongly still answers to the One whose name it borrowed. Whether the person in power believes it or not, that’s their business. Our business is to believe it and respond to circumstances as such.

📖 Memory Verse

By me kings reign, and princes decree justice.

(Proverbs 8:15)
🙏 Prayer

Dear God, let me honour the authority You have permitted, trusting that You alone hold every one of them accountable for how it is used. Amen.
