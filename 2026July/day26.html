July 26

Overcome Evil with Good

See that no one renders evil for evil to anyone, but always pursue what is good both for yourselves and for all. (1 Thessalonians 5:15)

When people treat us unfairly, as ordinary human beings, our first instinct is almost always to get even. We want them to feel the same pain they caused us. But, as God’s people, we are not ordinary people. Because of this, He calls us to a different response.

It is interesting that in Romans 12:19–21, the Bible informs us that vengeance belongs to the Lord. What then is our part? It is not to repay evil with evil but to overcome evil with good and (according to the apostle Peter) with blessing (1 Peter 3:9–11).

It is true that doing good is rarely easy. It is even harder when the offence is deep and fresh. But God does not ask us to wait for time to heal our wounds or design our response to offence based on its severity. He says, in effect, “Leave vengeance for Me and do good.”

The standard does not change simply because the offence or evil came from family members or close friends, neighbours, colleagues, or strangers. It is the same to all people. It is a sign that we trust God and believe that He will judge all things righteously. By so doing, believers represent God among people who believe in retaliation and an eye for an eye.

📖 Memory Verse

Be not over come of evil, but over come evil with good.

(Romans 12:21)
🙏 Prayer

Dear Lord, help me guard my heart from revenge, and teach me to reflect Your goodness even when I am treated unfairly. Amen.
