SEPTEMBER 9

I Love Him, I Love Him Not?

“My little children, let us not love in word or in tongue, but in deed and in truth. And by this we know that we are of the truth, and shall assure our hearts before Him.” (1 John 3:18–19)

Have you ever wondered, “Do I really love God, or am I just pretending?”

Maybe you worship, pray, read your Bible, serve, and say the right things, but somewhere inside, you wonder if your heart is actually in it. Perhaps you don’t always feel deeply connected to God and it makes you afraid. But loving God is not the same as constantly feeling emotional about God.

Human feelings change frequently. Some days we feel close to God; other days, we don’t. No wonder Jeremiah 17:9 insists that the human heart can be difficult to understand. We can misread our own emotions and even deceive ourselves. That’s why “What is the direction of my heart?” is a more important question than asking about what you feel. To which 1 John 3:18–19 responds that love is best expressed in truth and action.

So how do you know you’re not just pretending? For one, you care whether your love for God is genuine at all. A heart that is completely indifferent toward God usually isn’t troubled by the question.

You don’t have to manufacture affection for God to prove that you love Him. You don’t have to force yourself to cry during worship or create an emotional experience so you can feel like a “good Christian.” Instead, bring the honest heart you have to Him, saying, “God, I don’t know what I feel right now, but I want to love You. Help me love You genuinely.”

God searches the heart (Jeremiah 17:10). You don’t have to convince Him that you love Him. He even knows what you cannot fully understand about yourself. Therefore, instead of asking, “Do I feel like I love God enough?” perhaps you should ask instead, “Am I willing to come to Him honestly?” and “When I fail, do I still turn back to Him?” and “Do I desire to know Him, even when I don’t feel much?” and “Is His presence something I seek?” and “Am I becoming more willing to obey Him?”

You don’t have to pretend before the God who already knows your heart. Bring Him the real you, the confused you, the dry you, the doubtful you, the hungry for Him you, even the sinful you.

📖 MEMORY VERSE

(Jeremiah 17:9–10)
🙏 PRAYER
