SEPTEMBER 22

Religious Routine

“But you, when you pray, go into your room, and when you have shut your door, pray to your Father who is in the secret place; and your Father who sees in secret will reward you openly.” (Matthew 6:6)

There is a danger in religious routines: that someone prays every day, goes to church every Sunday, fasts, serves, and still misses God. It is not at all normal, but even when we do all the “right things”, it is not pleasing to God until it is done with the right heart.

The Lord Jesus had to say at one point (Matthew 15:8), “These people draw near with their mouth and honour Me with their lips, but their heart is far from Me.” What did He see, if not that worship had become automatic (and not in a good way): lips in constant motion and hearts in constant absence?

God preferred the temple doors closed over offerings given without heart. This is a clear indication of how serious He is about sincerity. Far more than the quantity of religious activities, the Lord is pleased with the quality of devotion. Therefore, a real pursuit of God happens in secret, with the Father, not for applause.

Unfortunately, many believers have traded intimacy for activity. They are busy doing things for God but have neglected being with God. This is a dangerous place to be because ministry without intimacy leads to burnout, pride, and ultimately, spiritual dryness.

Dear believer in Christ Jesus, prioritize fellowship with the Lord. Let your prayers, your giving, and your service flow from a heart that genuinely loves Him and not from a sense of religious obligation.

📖 MEMORY VERSE

(Isaiah 29:13)
🙏 PRAYER
Father, remove every empty routine and give me a heart that truly seeks You. Amen!
