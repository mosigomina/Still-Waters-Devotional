SEPTEMBER 2

Why So Fickle?

“Therefore, my beloved brethren, be ye stedfast, unmoveable, always abounding in the work of the Lord, forasmuch as ye know that your labour is not in vain in the Lord.” (1 Corinthians 15:58)

Inconsistency is a real thing. It is just as real to feel like being consistent is burdensome. But you have the right to handle this feeling from your end.

To deal with it, there are obvious considerations you must make. It is important to identify the presence of the issue, as this awareness gives you knowledge on what you are dealing with.

Upon identification, there will be a need to return to the throne of grace, as grace is the provision of God that makes it possible for one to stay and live in continuous fellowship with the Lord. One vital way to deal with this issue is to surround yourself with like-minds who will stir up the desire of God in you.

Among many things, they will also bring to your remembrance certain benefits and privileges of a believer who stays steady in their pursuit of the Lord. In all, you have to refuse to get tired of staying consistent, as choosing otherwise will lead to the loss of the profits it would have brought.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
And let us not be weary in well doing: for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not.
(Galatians 6:9)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, I learned that it is possible to stay steady in following You, grant me Your grace in Jesus name. Amen!
