SEPTEMBER 12

Finding Doubles for God

“‘Son of man, these men have set up idols in their hearts and put wicked stumbling blocks before their faces. Should I let them inquire of me at all?’” (Ezekiel 14:3)

Continuing from yesterday’s meditation on things capable of drawing your heart away from God, do you know that doppelgängers exist even for—better yet, against—God? Anything able to possess your allegiance and pound away at your devotion to God, having more of your attention than God does, is a God-double.

These things can be seemingly ordinary, like work: you get so busy, unable to yank yourself from it to learn from, pray to, worship, or even enjoy God. That work is a God-double. Remember Martha (Luke 10:40–42).

God-doubles can also be things that the Bible warns us against; they are especially these. Like the systems of this world: positions and dispositions, attitudes and attachments, actions and conduct that counter the concerns and commands of God. To esteem these things, Apostle James says, is to estrange God (James 4:4).

Today is a good day to take inventory of the things internal and at hand. What has your most attention most of the time? What is most responsible for your anxieties and excitements? Do you identify as something other than what you have learnt in Christ?

If it takes your mind away from God once and again, it is a God-double. If it saps sincerity and zeal towards God, it is a God-double. All such things are your enemy. Treat them as such because they have no other design than to draw you away from God, drag you down, and destroy you.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
“…for where your treasure is, there your heart [your wishes, your desires; that on which your life centres] will be also.”
(Matthew 6:21 AMP)
🙏 PRAYER
Father, thank You for reminding me today that many things can become substitutes for You in my heart and life. Help me dislodge those already rooted in my heart. It is You I want. Amen!
