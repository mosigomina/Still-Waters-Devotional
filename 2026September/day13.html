SEPTEMBER 13

Enjoying Earthly Things

“…but the worries of this life, the deceitfulness of wealth and the desires for other things come in and choke the word, making it unfruitful.” (Mark 4:19)

The earth is the Lord’s and the fullness thereof. He has granted us access to its many resources, seeing that He has made it good. But it is possible to become carried away by the pleasures of this world and forget Him, just as the parable of the sower would portray.

How then do we ensure we do not get carried away by the world’s vanities?

We do so by remembering that all that we have is from God (1 Timothy 6:17). He is the Source of all resources, and we should only look to the Creator, not the creation. This is so that even when those good things no longer come, we do not fidget unnecessarily but rather cast our hope on the Lord.

God is interested in giving us good things, but He doesn’t want those good things to have us. Therefore, we must ensure that our hearts do not forget their Lord in the midst of plenty.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
So whether you eat or drink or whatever you do, do it all for the glory of God.
(1 Corinthians 10:31)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, help me to never be choked by the pleasures of this world and may those things You give me never be the reason I turn away from You. Amen!
