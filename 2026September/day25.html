SEPTEMBER 25

Draining Relationships

“One who walks with wise people will be wise, but a companion of fools will suffer harm.” (Proverbs 13:20)

Yesterday, we offered instances of Christian communities wearing down and wearing out our desire and love for God. We are going to double-click on that today.

For a way of life that draws much of its power and effectiveness from being loved by and loving God, Christianity is strict regarding how we relate to others. For example, in 1 Corinthians 5:9–10, we are instructed to never associate with sexually immoral Christians. In 2 Thessalonians 3:6, the embargo extends to Christians who are undisciplined and disorderly.

Even Apostle John, the one we call the Apostle of Love, commands in his second epistle (verse 9–10): don’t receive into your house anyone who does not remain in the teaching of Christ; don’t even receive them! Why? Because such people (and the relationships we have with them) drain spiritual hunger. Essentially, they corrode and erode our desire and love for God.

How do they do so?

Some of them make us question the existence of God, not often directly but with insinuations. Others make us question God’s power or God’s wisdom or God’s goodness, three important legs supporting why we have received Christ’s salvation. Others even endear the world to us, inadvertently rendering us enemies of God (James 4:4).

What do we do with such people who drain spiritual hunger and passion and fire? We distance ourselves and commit them to God in prayer, trusting Him to draw them closer to Himself and away from the path they are pulling us towards.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
“…whoever does the will of My Father who is in heaven, he is My brother, and sister, and mother.”
(Matthew 12:50)
🙏 PRAYER
Father, thank You for reminding me that some relationships can leak away my spiritual hunger and desire for You. Please, let me identify them; and grant me courage to distance myself while I await further instructions from You. Amen!
