SEPTEMBER 1

Spiritual Inconsistency

“I know thy works, that thou art neither cold nor hot: I would thou wert cold or hot. So then because thou art lukewarm, and neither cold nor hot, I will spue thee out of my mouth.” (Revelation 3:15–16)

God desires that we follow Him steadily and continually, yet due to our physical state, we sometimes slip away, and this is not often because we don’t love the Lord, but sometimes we forget that there may be dangers attached to this slipping away.

We become vulnerable to the subtle desires of the flesh, which could have been tamed. With this also comes the break in communication with the Father, which often leads to the guilt of lack of commitment to the daily charge of following the Lord given in Luke 9:23.

These dangers can creep in and distort a believer’s pursuit of the Lord. With consideration, we can make the right turn and avoid these consequences.

According to Revelation 3:15–16, it becomes more of a matter of eternal consequence for not being on fire for the Lord, which makes it a point of duty for a believer and not an option. If we will follow after the Lord and not miss out on the promises and provisions, then we have to be steady and continue our following.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
A double minded man is unstable in all his ways.
(James 1:8)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, help me to avoid the dangers of spiritual inconsistency by being fervent in Jesus name. Amen!
