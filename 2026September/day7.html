SEPTEMBER 7

Pleasure in Loving God

“You will show me the path of life; in Your presence is fullness of joy; at Your right hand are pleasures forevermore.” (Psalm 16:11)

Human love can be so messy. It usually depends on people liking us back, or approving of us. But loving God is completely different. Because He never changes and is the source of everything, loving Him brings a steady joy that doesn’t depend on anyone else.

The Bible points right to this in Psalm 16:11, which says that being close to God brings true fullness of joy and “eternal pleasures at your right hand.” No, the pleasure being referenced here is not the quick emotional high we know, but a deep, soul satisfaction that comes simply from adoring our Creator. In Psalm 73:25–26, Asaph puts it into words we can all feel: “Whom have I in heaven but you? And earth has nothing I desire besides you.” He shows us that the best part of loving God is God Himself. He isn’t just someone who hands out blessings; He is the ultimate reward.

Of course, loving Him isn’t always easy or comfortable. As Hebrews 12:11 reminds us, God’s discipline can actually hurt or feel heavy in the moment as He shapes our hearts. But that training eventually grows a lasting harvest of peace and goodness in our lives.

At the end of the day, the pleasure of loving God is about resting in who He is without needing human applause or a reward. When we treasure Him above everything else, we find the deepest joy our hearts could ever ask for.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Now no chastening seems to be joyful for the present, but painful; nevertheless, afterward it yields the peaceable fruit of righteousness to those who have been trained by it.
(Hebrews 12:11)
🙏 PRAYER
Teach me to experience the deepest pleasure of loving You. Amen!
