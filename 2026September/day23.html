SEPTEMBER 23

Don’t Want! Don’t Care!

“But those who wait on the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings like eagles, they shall run and not be weary, they shall walk and not faint.” (Isaiah 40:31)

There are days when praying feels like a heavy exercise and Bible study (even Bible reading) is like the driest event there is, with worship bringing nothing to us, not even those chills we get when the music is slow. But we have good news for you! God is aware of those days. Plus, He still calls us to Him in them.

Spiritual maturity is many things. One thing it is not is how you always feel. One thing it is is what you do when you don’t feel like it. After all, feelings are fickle and unreliable. They change with our mood, our circumstances, and even the weather. But obedience is a choice that transcends feelings.

Luke 22:42–44 says, “Father, if You are willing, remove this cup from Me. Nevertheless, not My will, but Yours, be done... And being in agony, He was praying more earnestly...” The Lord Jesus was exhausted, in agony, and didn’t “feel like it.” But He still chose obedience. He prayed anyway. He surrendered anyway. Feelings didn’t lead Him; what led Him was the Father’s will.

If the Son of God Himself had to push through in prayer when He didn’t feel like it, how much more do we need to discipline ourselves to do the same?

The believer who prays when they don’t feel like praying, reads the Word when it feels dry, and worships when the heart is heavy, is building spiritual muscle that will carry them through the tough seasons.

Therefore, dear believer in Christ Jesus, let the Word of God motivate you daily to interface with the Lord. Don’t wait for the feeling to come. Obey your way into it. The feeling often follows the act of obedience, not the other way around.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Father, if You are willing, remove this cup from Me. Nevertheless, not My will, but Yours, be done.
(Luke 22:42)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, help me to obey and seek You even when I don’t feel like it. Amen!
