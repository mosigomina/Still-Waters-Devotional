SEPTEMBER 14

Worldly Pleasures

“By faith Moses, when he was come to years, refused to be called the son of Pharaoh’s daughter; choosing rather to suffer affliction with the people of God, than to enjoy the pleasures of sin for a season; esteeming the reproach of Christ greater riches than the treasures in Egypt: for he had respect unto the recompense of the reward.” (Hebrews 11:24–26)

God is our greatest good. It means that outside Him, nothing else has the ability to fully satisfy our hearts, and those who determine to chase after other things will end up hurt and confused.

But this isn’t to deny that other things are attractive. It was recorded that when Eve beheld the fruit of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, she found it pleasing to the eyes. There are things in this world that delight our souls, namely: comfort, wealth, and riches, amongst others; however, when we make them our greatest pursuit, they turn around and become snares.

The Lord is more than enough reward. A true Christian delights in having Him. Nothing else should delight our souls, and we must never find pleasure anywhere else. If your soul does not seek the Lord first and most of all, it is unhealthy.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
As the deer pants for streams of water, so my soul pants for you, my God.
(Psalms 42:1)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, help me not to love the world and its pleasures but to only delight in You. Amen!
