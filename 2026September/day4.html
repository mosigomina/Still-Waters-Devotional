SEPTEMBER 4

Facing Persecution

“Blessed are those who have been persecuted for the sake of righteousness, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven. Blessed are you when people insult you and persecute you, and falsely say all kinds of evil against you because of Me. Rejoice and be glad, for your reward in heaven is great; for in the same way they persecuted the prophets who were before you.” (Matthew 5:10–12)

Persecution is the suffering and torment a person is sentenced to on account of a way of life or manner of thinking. In our time, persecution can be loud and unabashed, but it can also be creeping sometimes: snide and spiteful words spoken, internet comments designed to crush, and so on. It’s the world system’s way of trying to convince you that a godly life is not worth it. A very false claim.

The reward that Christ Jesus promises those who are persecuted, not for indulging in sin, for living righteous lives, is that the kingdom of heaven is theirs. They are not going to lose being partakers, so long as they continue in godliness.

While Scripture unravels different provisions the Lord gives to help us stand as gallant soldiers in the face of the enemy’s advances, hurtful and painful as they may be, today’s devotional wants to draw to mind one thing those who are persecuted can do. By fixing our minds on the hope of what we have been promised, we will find joy even in the midst of persecution.

You can trust this to be possible when you read certain accounts of the disciples of old. Acts 5:41 and 13:50–52 prove this to us. As absurd as it may sound to others, you who plan to stand strong, fix your heart on these words: Rejoice, then again, rejoice, for the sufferings of this world are nothing compared to the glory we will find when Christ returns.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Indeed, all who desire to live godly in Christ Jesus will be persecuted.
(2 Timothy 3:12)
🙏 PRAYER
Hard as it may be, dear Lord, help me remember my hope that is built in You and remember always that though my weeping may last through the night, there is an ultimate joy You have in store for me. Amen!
