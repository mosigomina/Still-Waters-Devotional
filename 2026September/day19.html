SEPTEMBER 19

Spiritual Hunger as Feeling vs. Cultivation

“No one can come to Me unless the Father who sent Me draws him; and I will raise him up at the last day.” (John 6:44)

The thought of a person forgetting to eat for an entire day might sound unbelievable, but it can happen on busy days or during illness. Therefore, hunger in itself is not evil. A healthy appetite is often a good sign that a person is well, but our well-being does not end with the call to eat. What we consume is equally important to our health.

So it is with spiritual hunger. God is the One who initiates this hunger. Just as He called the Israelites out of Egypt through Moses to worship Him in the wilderness, He also draws us to Himself. As the Lord Jesus said, “No one can come to Me unless the Father who sent Me draws him” (John 6:44).

However, just as you would not eat rotten food or consume poison simply because you are hungry, it is important to feed your spiritual hunger correctly. We must sow rightly and cultivate the right practices, rightly dividing the Word of truth (2 Timothy 2:15), declaring the Word, and responding to it through active obedience.

This is a reasonable response to the call of God—not turning to idols or building a golden calf as the Israelites did. When our hunger for God is rightly directed and nourished by His Word, we will surely find the God whom our hearts desire.

(Hosea 10:12)
