SEPTEMBER 15

Desire in Distraction

“Set your mind and keep focused habitually on the things above [the heavenly things], not on things that are on the earth [which have only temporal value].” (Colossians 3:2)

Saint Augustine once said, “You have made us for yourself, oh Lord, and our heart is restless until it rests in You.” Even as believers in Christ Jesus, we are prone to wander: pandering hearts that desire everything else except their God. It remains a continual struggle for the one in the flesh to be fully fixed on the Lord Jesus, but it is not impossible.

We have the faithful testimonies of patriarchs of old, who, even in their feebleness, continued to reach out to the Lord. They did not achieve this by idle wishing but by wrestling against their tendencies. No wonder the Lord later tells us that if we must follow after Him, we must carry our crosses daily.

Our strength will fail; but through prayers, holding fast to God’s word, and fellowshipping with fellow believers, we will align our hearts to God’s heart, so that His strength and passions flow into our own. Therefore, we must learn to incorporate our worship in our daily life. If we must become, then we must behold.
