SEPTEMBER 21

Where Is Your Hunger?

“Why are you cast down, O my soul? And why are you disquieted within me? Hope in God, for I shall yet praise Him for the help of His countenance.” (Psalm 42:5)

Have you ever felt like the fire you once had for God and the eagerness to pray, to open your Bible, to worship, and now it feels dry? Dear believer in Christ Jesus, you’re not alone. Even strong believers go through seasons where their “hunger for God” seems to disappear.

The book of Revelation 2:4–5 says, “Nevertheless I have this against you, that you have left your first love. Remember, therefore, from where you have fallen; repent and do the first works...” What was happening here? Was the Lord Jesus angry at the Ephesian church for working hard? No! He was concerned because their love grew cold.

The hunger left and they became dull of hearing; clearly, the effect the Word of God ought to have in their lives wasn’t coming into play. And the solution the Lord had for them was, “Remember, Repent, Return.” In other words, go back to what you did at first.

Dear believer in Christ Jesus, it is important to understand that in your season of dryness, working alone on this journey can sometimes be dangerous. That’s why the gathering (fellowship) of brethren is important. Scripture says, “As iron sharpens iron.” When we isolate ourselves, the enemy takes advantage, but when we surround ourselves with fellow believers, we find strength, encouragement, and accountability to press on.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Nevertheless I have this against you, that you have left your first love. Remember, therefore, from where you have fallen; repent and do the first works...
(Revelation 2:4–5)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, restore my first love and renew my hunger for You. Amen!
