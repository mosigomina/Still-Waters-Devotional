SEPTEMBER 17

One Focus

“I press on to reach the end of the race and receive the heavenly prize for which God, through Christ Jesus is calling us.” (Philippians 3:14)

There is a saying that a person born with countless desires rarely pursues any one of them with conviction. A life without conviction is unstable, reactive, and hollow. It is like a jellyfish tossed here and there by any idea or event. A doubled-minded person is unstable in all his ways (James 1:8). The only way out of this dilemma is to have a mind convinced and focused on a few or a single thing. The Bible tells us that all our desires and focus should be on God.

Someone might ask, “How can I make God my central desire?” The Bible tells us it is by fixing our eyes on the Lord Jesus Christ and on the things above where He dwells (Hebrews 12:2, Colossians 3:1).

Knowing that our desires are to be channelled towards God frees us from so many want-to-be’s, like: “I want to be rich” (which Paul tells us that those who want to be rich pierce themselves with many sorrows); “I want to be liked and accepted by people”; “I want to be perceived as wise, intelligent, and successful.” A God-focused life frees us from the expectations of people. Our lives become stable because God has become our number one consideration and nothing else matters.

To live an effective Christian life, a life not weighed down by sin, worries and fear, our focus has to stay on the Lord. The Bible tells us that the Lord keeps him in perfect peace whose mind is stayed on Him because he trusts in Him.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
The conclusion, when everything has been heard, is: fear God and keep His commandments, because this applies to every person.
(Ecclesiastes 12:13)
🙏 PRAYER
My Father, I ask in the name of your son Jesus, that give me the grace to continually fix my eyes on the Lord Jesus Christ the author and the finisher of my faith. Amen!
