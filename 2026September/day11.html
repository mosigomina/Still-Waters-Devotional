SEPTEMBER 11

Ambitions and Dreams

“And Jabez called on the God of Israel saying, ‘Oh, that You would bless me indeed, and enlarge my territory, that Your hand would be with me, and that You would keep me from evil, that I may not cause pain!’ So God granted him what he requested.” (1 Chronicles 4:10)

Can I genuinely desire God while still having other ambitions, dreams, and pleasures? If the answer is no, then, as believers, we must get a noose for these things and hang them quickly. If the answer is yes, why do we feel guilty about some of them sometimes? Let us look to the Bible for answers, starting at the very beginning.

In Genesis, Adam and Eve desired to have things God had not availed to them, namely, wisdom and sovereignty. It was an ambition for self-rule and self-exaltation; for steering their lives themselves and swerving whenever and however they wanted. Why should they, having intuition and choice, choose to stay attached to God’s chariot, never mind that He is our Maker and has intentions for our creation?

In the final analysis, it was not simply that our first parents wanted something. Rather, it was that what they wanted was other. In other words, other than God; other than what God supported and supplied.

If we say we have other ambitions, other dreams, and other pleasures, that is itself a problem. Because, other how? Other than the ambitions, and dreams, and pleasures that God has designed for us? Clearly, the answer is no. No, you cannot genuinely desire God while still having ‘other’ ambitions, dreams, and pleasures.

How do you know something is other? It is other when it draws you away from God.

🙏 PRAYER
