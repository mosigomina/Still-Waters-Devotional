SEPTEMBER 30

Desiring God Every Day

“If I have the gift of prophecy and know all mysteries and all knowledge, and if I have all faith so as to remove mountains, but do not have love, I am nothing.” (1 Corinthians 13:2)

This month’s devotional has focused on desiring and loving God, counting the cost of this decision, pursuing Him, setting our hearts aright, sustaining the fire, loving Him in the company of other believers in Christ, and nurturing the life that loving God produces. What better way to tie things up than to double-click on why our love for God must not fizzle out or become something we feign?

In our core reference, the good apostle, Paul, works out the value of true, God-approved love. According to him, to give one’s body in martyrdom or all of our possessions is nothing if the underlying goal is not love. As if to say that if the evidence for our love for God can be wrapped up only in our charitableness and outward choices but not in an increasing personification of who God is, it counts for less than we think.

In the final analysis, the way to know that our love for God is not just talk is that we are actually adjusting, changing from being self-centred and self-absorbed people to disciples of Jesus Christ. That our ears are open to Him (Psalm 40:6), as are our lives. That God, the One we say we desire above all else, is our Lord and Master.

It means that we are always open to repentance, eager to be corrected by Him when we are wrong—eager also to be praised by Him when we do His will. It means that on a daily basis, it is from God, especially through His Word (the Bible), that we draw meaning and understanding.

A true love for God changes a person. Even for the ones who are not completely ‘head over heels in love’ with Him, they look to Him for everything: support, encouragement, consolation, correction, food, money, healing, grace, pampering, life. And they look forward to meeting Him when they close their eyes in death.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
I love the Lord because he hears my voice and my prayer for mercy. Because he bends down to listen, I will pray as long as I have breath!
(Psalm 116:1–2 NLT)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, thank You for a month spent enjoying You in devotion. Please, help me: let my love and desire for You never burn out; let it grow stronger and stronger and stronger until that great Day. Amen!
