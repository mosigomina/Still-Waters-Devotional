SEPTEMBER 28

What About Obedience and Holiness?

“I have taken off my robe—must I put it on again? I have washed my feet—must I soil them again?” (Song of Songs 5:3)

Are men naturally polygamous? Some people would have us believe so. They argue that a promiscuous man is a typical man, and the man who puts forward fidelity as his lifestyle is not faithful to his nature.

All such arguments, no matter how very much they function or malfunction in real life, have a basis: what people understand a man to be. Either a human being capable of moral failure now and again, or a twisted, unscrupulous thing permanently tramping towards perversion.

In Christ, we also have an understanding based on the words of our Saviour, the Lord Jesus. Namely, that the person that loves God (in God’s eyes) is the one that obeys His commands (John 14:15).

It is an absolute condition with no accommodation for modification. It applies much in like manner to holiness: because God is holy, the person who loves God and is interested in a continuing relationship with Him must be holy (1 Peter 1:16).

These work in a cycle: because we obey God and live as those set apart, pure, and distinct in conduct so as to reflect the character of God, we will find our love for Him increasing, fleshing out, and flourishing. And because we are loving Him more and more, we will enjoy obeying Him and imitating His holiness fully and effortlessly.

Let us love God. Let us not make a habit out of presenting to Him a love that is sickly and dying, poked to tatters with disobedience and excuses for desecration and defilement.
