SEPTEMBER 20

Spiritual Discipline

“So He Himself often withdrew into the wilderness and prayed.” (Luke 5:16)

Yesterday, we saw that spiritual hunger should not stop at feeling alone; it should lead us into cultivation. As we read in Galatians 6:8, those who sow to the flesh, living only to satisfy their sinful nature, will reap destruction and death. But those who sow to the Spirit, living to please the Spirit of God, will reap everlasting life.

To do this, we require a great deal of spiritual discipline. This means being intentional and consistent in practices such as studying the Word, meditation, prayer, and fellowship with other believers.

It is important not to regard these practices as mere clichés simply because we have heard them repeatedly. They have the power to draw us closer to the heart of God, deepen our spiritual growth, and strengthen our ability to live according to His will.

The key Person here is the Spirit of God. Live by the Spirit. Allow the Spirit to direct your life, and you will not gratify the desires of the flesh. Instead, as you walk with Him, your desire for God will grow even stronger.
