SEPTEMBER 3

Self-Denial and Personal Pleasure

“If anyone comes to Me and does not hate his [own] father and mother [in the sense of indifference to or relative disregard for them in comparison with his attitude toward God] and [likewise] his wife and children and brothers and sisters—[yes] and even his own life also—he cannot be My disciple. Whoever does not persevere and carry his own cross and come after (follow) Me cannot be My disciple.” (Luke 14:26–27 AMPC)

God is not averse to pleasure. What He opposes is the overindulgence that makes pleasure the directive and direction of all our thoughts and actions. This may look seemingly contradictory because, on the one hand, we are told to deny self, and on the other hand, we are also told by Scripture that we can enjoy this life. It would pay us to know what is being said, not what is not, and run with that.

Not only is God not opposed to our pleasure, He also is the one who makes and supplies us with all things to enjoy. But it is like Paul says, our hopes are not to be fixed on these things nor should our ultimate fulfilment. The place to fix them is in God, always. It is the gratification and push to be satisfied above all else, even at the cost of our daily walk with the Lord, that is problematic.

It is important to know that we are not called to be slaves of our own desires. This is the stroke many seem not to make when painting pictures of hopes and wishes. Th apostle, James, addresses this in his letter when he tells the church that they ask and have not because they seek to spend it on their lusts (James 4:3).

We are not to become as fools, given over to every thought our hearts conjure. Bear in mind, the flesh will continue to wage war over the spirit. Today’s topic is not even encouraging this in the slightest. It is, however, saying that every gift that comes from the Lord, materials inclusive, can be enjoyed while giving glory to God.

Enjoyment is not sinful, but it is not our utmost focus. God is our focus, fulfilment and reward. What we do, we do foremost for Him.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
As for the rich in this world, charge them not to be proud and arrogant and contemptuous of others, nor to set their hopes on uncertain riches, but on God, Who richly and ceaselessly provides us with everything for [our] enjoyment.
(1 Timothy 6:17 AMPC)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, thank You for all good gifts You have been providing for me. I ask that You help me not to be afraid to indulge them and that You also help me be wise enough to not appraise them more than is necessary. That You alone would be my glory. Amen!
