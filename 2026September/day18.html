SEPTEMBER 18

God-Centred Living

“Dear Friends, you always followed my instructions when I was with you, and now that I am away, it is even more important. Work hard to show the results of your salvation, obeying God with deep reverence and fear.” (Philippians 2:13)

The word ‘slave’ is a repulsive and emotional rallying word that nobody wants to be called or identified with. A slave is a person whose life is determined and designed by his master. We are God’s slaves because we have been bought by God with the blood of the Lord Jesus as the payment for our redemption. Therefore, God has the right to design and direct our lives as He pleases. This life designed and directed by God is what we refer to as God-centred living.

You may ask, “What does this life designed by God look like? Welcome to class!

God-centred living is a life of obedience. It is the life demonstrated to us by our Lord Jesus Christ who obeyed the Father even when obedience involved pain and suffering. Job 22:23 says, “Receive Instruction from His mouth and place His sayings in your heart.” Psalms 119:4 tells us that God has commanded that His precepts be diligently kept. Therefore, whatever God says in the Bible must be obeyed. That is God-centred living.

God-centred living is a life of trust, the life that believes whatever God says. We see this in Abraham’s life. He believed God’s promise of a son when he was an old man and he received Isaac at 100 years old (Genesis 18:14, Hebrews 11:11). He believed God even when what God said was contrary to reality. This is the kind of life that God desires: a life that believes and trust even when it looks foolish and unrealistic, simply because God said so.

In conclusion, God-centred living is the life lived in total obedience to God’s instruction and the life that trusts in God fully.

(Proverbs 3:5)
