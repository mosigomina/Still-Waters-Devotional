SEPTEMBER 16

Knowing God, Loving God

“If you want to be my disciple, you must, by comparison, hate everyone else—your father and mother, wife and children, brothers and sisters—yes, even your own life. Otherwise, you cannot be my disciple. And if you do not carry your own cross and follow me, you cannot be my disciple.” (Luke 14:26–27)

The Bible tells us the importance of knowledge when it says, “My people perish because they lack Knowledge” (Hosea 4:6). A person can know many things, like sciences, economics, psychology, philosophy, and so on; but if he lacks the knowledge of the Holy one, he is considered by God to be ignorant and simple.

True knowledge is the knowledge of the living God. This knowledge is transformative; it causes a change in a person’s entity. We shall consider the most visible and transformative change that occurs in a person as they grow in their knowledge of God.

This transformation is the change in nature. As a person begins to learn of God and know God, his nature is changed; he begins to look like the Lord Jesus Christ in actions and thoughts.

The Bible tells us to let the words of the Lord dwell in not richly (Colossians 3:16); these words transform our mind, which is the seat of our thoughts, and our actions proceeds from there also. The Bible tells us that we were created to conform to the image of the Lord Jesus Christ (Romans 8:19). This can happen only if we grow in our knowledge of God and the Lord Jesus Christ.

Other characters also spring from this transformation: we begin to love God and people around us with a sincere and pure heart; carrying our cross becomes easier (because of our knowledge of the greatness of God); trusting Him becomes easier. Our fears and worries begin to fade, and our faith and hope become sturdier. We become pleasing to God, for we please God by our faith in Him.

📖 MEMORY VERSE

(Matthew 6:33)
