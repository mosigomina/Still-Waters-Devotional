SEPTEMBER 5

Self-Love vs God-Love

“Then said Jesus unto his disciples, If any man will come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow me. For whosoever will save his life shall lose it: and whosoever will lose his life for my sake shall find it.” (Matthew 16:24–25)

We are already faced daily with the decision that shows how much we love God and prioritise Him in our life, and that alone brings about the question of how we get to balance the desires of God knowing that we also love ourselves.

In following the Lord, you would need to know that, like every relationship, it would require that you put first the one with whom you are in a relationship. It is most often by doing so that we find peace.

Also, in our relationship with the Lord, the way is by making God’s desire the first that brings you peace; in addition to that, you need to make it the major part of your sense of fulfilment.

In order to make it possible that God’s desire takes place ahead of our comfort and convenience, there is a need to act on the Word of God, many times not based on our perspective but as it was literally written. In Matthew 16:24, the Lord Jesus made denying oneself of certain comfort a formal requirement in following Him.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
For whosoever will save his life shall lose it: and whosoever will lose his life for my sake shall find it.
(Matthew 16:24–25)
🙏 PRAYER
May I find peace in prioritising Your desires as I live day by day in Jesus name. Amen!
