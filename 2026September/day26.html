SEPTEMBER 26

Qualities of Spiritual Companions

“There are many who say, ‘You can trust me!’ But can they be trusted?” (Proverbs 20:6 CEV)

The believer’s walk with God is a delicate one, not because the path is thorny or our Master-Companion is testy. Rather, we are feeble, having hearts that wrinkle when stressed and faiths that ruffle when strained. Therefore, in addition to our Lord and Master, it helps to have other believers walking adjacent to us.

The Bible has much to say about the companions we can walk with on this eternal path. One, they must be in Christ, without which there is no fellowship whatsoever. But they must also have a heart that wants to please God (Hebrews 11:6). That is, they have to want it; it might be difficult to maintain this desire, but it must be there.

Another quality is devotion to the teachings of the Apostles of the Lamb (Acts 2:42). Which means they also have to believe in the prophets. Essentially, they cannot be people who pick and choose which part of the Holy Scripture to believe.

Then, they must be faithful. The path of conformity to Christ is one that requires a person to live by faith, to be doers of the work of God and men, and not slothful in business.

All in all, on the path to life, God has decided that we must have other companions apart from Him. All such people must have their gazes on the Lord, willing to adjust when necessary, pushing to be disciples of Christ indeed.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
They spent their time in learning from the apostles, taking part in the fellowship, and sharing in the fellowship meals and the prayers.
(Acts 2:42 GNT)
🙏 PRAYER
Good Father, thank You for the gift of companionship on this path towards You. Lord, grant me faithful allies and confidantes, and make me the same for others. Amen!
