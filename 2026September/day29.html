SEPTEMBER 29

Relationships, Purpose, and Service

“Many waters cannot quench love; rivers cannot sweep it away. If one were to give all the wealth of one’s house for love, it would be utterly scorned.” (Song of Songs 8:7)

The love of God stimulates strong sensations in us. Just thinking about Him switches the pedals of our hearts, making them beat away as we anticipate His presence and fullness forever. Stopping here, mesmerised and spellbound, is like strutting around the edges of God’s love garden, never moving in to savour it in full.

Loving God is most sonorous to Him when we submit to the stipulations of love. When this desire calls us—nay, commands us—to love our brothers and sisters in Christ, and love them with a love more than words (1 John 4:20–21), we are happy to obey.

When this love places a sign on natural blood relationships (as in Luke 14:26), we join in to chorus, “Unless born again, hated in comparison with my love for Christ and my companions in Christ.”

When this love urges that we love and preach and live the Gospel of the Lord Jesus, we do so, shouting into every ear that for our sins, Christ died, was buried and resurrected, ascended and was glorified, and will return to take us home.

Because we love God, the will of the Father is not something we join other people to wait for, but something we participate in to discharge and dispense.

In short, a person that desires another would want to do their bidding. When it is to serve, they serve them. When it is to wait, they wait on them. The fulfilment of a lover is in the satisfaction of their beloved. Because we love God, our relationships, purpose, and service are defined.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
And this commandment we have from Him, that the one who loves God must also love his brother and sister.
(1 John 4:21)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, thank You for loving me. Help me submit to the obligations of love in loving Your people and serving them and You. Amen!
