July 31

The Kingdom That Will Never End

And the kingdom shall not be left to other people; it shall break in pieces and consume all these kingdoms, and it shall stand forever. (Daniel 2:44)

Every nation we will ever serve has an expiration date; only one Kingdom does not.

Daniel knew this better than most. He served Babylon, then Medo–Persia, with skill and integrity his entire life, rising to the highest offices a foreign king could offer. And yet it was this same Daniel who, in chapter two, told Nebuchadnezzar that every empire, including his, would eventually be broken and replaced by a Kingdom that would never end.

Yet, the fact that Daniel knew that Babylon’s days were numbered did no make him serve it any less. When he served, he did so fully and faithfully, all the while acknowledging that he belonged to another place.

That is the model for everything this month has argued. We pray for our nation, submit to its authority, work for its good, and stand against its evil, not because it is permanent, but because God placed us here for this time. Isaiah 9:6–7 and Luke 1:33 point us to the throne that never ends, the government that will never be overturned, fulfilled in Christ. And if you have never recited the declaration of Revelation 11:15, now is the time to do so: the kingdoms of this world become the Kingdom of our Lord!

So we hold two truths together until that day. Full engagement with the nation we are in; full allegiance to the Kingdom that is coming. There is no contradiction between the two: one is here now, the other is established in eternity, with only time between us and it.

📖 Memory Verse

And the kingdoms of this world have become the kingdoms of our Lord and of His Christ.

(Revelation 11:15)
🙏 Prayer

Dear God, let me serve where You have placed me without losing sight of where it is all going. I belong, finally, to the Kingdom that will never end. Amen.
