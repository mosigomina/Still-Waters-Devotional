July 23

Expose the Works of Darkness

Have no fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather expose them. (Ephesians 5:11)

It is very tempting to read the first part of our Bible reference and run with only it. We would then preach it to other believers in Christ: have no fellowship with darkness, avoid it, stay clean, keep your own hands unstained.

This is a manageable position, to be honest. It is a safe and cushy position. Except, the second half of the statement asks us for something that is neither safe nor cushy. Expose the works of darkness, it says.

The Greek word behind ‘expose’ means to bring something hidden into the light; to convict; to name a wrong so clearly that it can no longer hide behind ambiguity. One commentator explained that it was a duty to have nothing to do with the deeds of darkness, yes, but also an obligation to reprimand them.

If we only avoid the darkness, it would be undisturbed, free to keep operating wherever no one is willing to name it. But because the night is far gone and the day is at hand, we are to cast off the works of darkness and put on the armour of light (Romans 13:12).

Keep in mind what Isaiah 5:20 says: that there are people who call evil good and good evil, who confuse light and darkness. Even these must be confronted and exposed to the light. As believers in Christ, we are neither here to make people comfortable nor cushion darkness, whatever form it takes. Let us make it our aim to expose darkness, so that the light that Christ brings can do its work (John 3:20-21).

📖 Memory Verse

Have no fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather expose them.

(Ephesians 5:11)
🙏 Prayer

Dear God, give me the courage to do more than avoid darkness. Let me name it clearly when You call me to, even when silence would be easier. Amen.
