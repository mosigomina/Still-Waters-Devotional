July 21

You Are the Light of the World

“You are the light of the world. A city that is set on a hill cannot be hidden.” (Matthew 5:14)

The Lord Jesus declared that those who follow Him are the light of the world. What does light do? It dispels darkness, reveals truth, and provides direction. As believers in Christ, we are called to reflect the character of Christ through our words, actions, and lifestyle, letting our good works shine before others so that God is glorified (Matthew 5:16).

What this looks like is what Philippians 2:15-16 specifies: that we shine as lights in the midst of a crooked and perverse generation, holding fast to the word of life, even while everything around us grows darker. That is the picture for us today.

God has called us to shine in our homes, workplaces, schools, churches, and communities, even with corruption, insecurity, moral decline, and hopelessness around us. Rather than contributing to the darkness through dishonesty, bitterness, or compromise, God’s people are to demonstrate integrity, love, justice, and hope, pointing others to Christ through both word and example.

Also, the light is not permitted to be simply quiet and private. We are called to arise and shine because the glory of the Lord has risen upon us, visible enough for nations to see (Isaiah 60:1-3). It is those that lead others to righteousness that shine like the stars forever (Daniel 12:3).

It is when believers faithfully proclaim the gospel and live lives that honour God that they become beacons of hope to their nation and generation. Every nation needs Christians who will not hide their light but will boldly shine for Christ, bringing transformation wherever God has placed them. Let us be such Christians.

📖 Memory Verse

Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.

(Matthew 5:16)
🙏 Prayer

Father, let Your light shine through me. Help me to be a godly example in my words, actions, and character. Amen.
