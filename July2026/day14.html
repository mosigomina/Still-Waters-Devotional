July 14

Give to Caesar What Belongs to Caesar

Render therefore unto Caesar the things which are Caesar’s; and unto God the things that are God’s. (Matthew 22:21)

In Matthew 22, the Pharisees handed the Lord Jesus a coin to trap Him. If He said the tax was unlawful, He would be branded a rebel against Rome. If He said it was lawful, He would lose the crowd who hated paying it. Either answer would cost Him.

The Lord asked a simple question instead: whose image is on this coin? Caesar’s, they answered. The coin carried Caesar’s likeness because Caesar had minted it. It belonged to him the way anything stamped with a maker’s image belongs to its maker.

The phrase “render to Caesar” gets misused often, stretched to justify all kinds of compromise in the name of civic duty. But the Lord Jesus was specific. According to Him, Caesar gets what bears his image, a coin he made and stamped with his own face; God gets what bears His image, which is every human soul, made and stamped by Him long before any government existed (Genesis 1:27).

These claims run along entirely different lines. Paying tax to a government is giving Caesar his coin back. It says nothing about who owns your conscience, your worship, or your obedience to God, because none of those things were ever Caesar’s to begin with. It must be the same in ordinary life. You ought to pay tax and other legal dues, because those collecting it are serving a purpose permitted by God Himself.

📖 Memory Verse

Render therefore unto Caesar the things which are Caesar’s; and unto God the things that are God’s.

(Matthew 22:21)
🙏 Prayer

Dear God, let me give to my nation what genuinely belongs to it, while keeping clear in my heart what has always and only belonged to You. Amen.
