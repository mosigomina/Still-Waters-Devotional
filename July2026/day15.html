July 15

Give Honour to Whom Honour is Due

Render therefore to all their dues... honour to whom honour. (Romans 13:7)

Have you ever paid attention to what the apostle Peter does in 1 Peter 2:17? In a single verse, he lays out four different instructions side by side, and none of them are interchangeable with the others.

This precision is the point. Honour for everyone is broad and general, the basic dignity owed to every person made in God’s image. Love for the brotherhood is warmer and closer, reserved for fellow believers. Fear belongs to God alone, a category nothing else shares. And honour for the king sits in its own place too, neither collapsed into general courtesy nor elevated into the reverence owed only to God.

What about Proverbs 24:21 which pairs fearing the Lord with honouring the king in the same breath, while warning against association with those given to change, restless rebellion against settled order? The two postures sit together comfortably because they were never competing for the same level of devotion.

We see the same precision applied in 1 Timothy 6:1 to servants under harsh masters, counting them worthy of honour, not because the master has necessarily earned it, but because honour given rightly protects the name of God being represented through that conduct.

God does not want us flattening everyone and everything into one response when it comes to honour. In private and public life, let us make it our aim to fear God, love our brethren, and honour our leaders.

📖 Memory Verse

Honour all men. Love the brotherhood. Fear God. Honour the king.

(1 Peter 2:17)
🙏 Prayer

Dear God, teach me to give each person and each authority their rightly weighted due, honouring rightly without confusing what belongs only to You. Amen.
