July 18

Righteousness Exalts a Nation

When the righteous increase, the people rejoice, But when a wicked man rules, people groan. (Proverbs 29:2)

What qualifies as righteousness, after new birth, is the uncompromising and non-negotiable approach towards the law of God. It is the direction and foundation.

For a nation to enjoy peace and prosperity and be reckoned in God’s sight as good, righteousness has to be the order of the day. It is the people who honour God and His instruction that will look out for one another, exhibit and appropriate wisdom, and shun temptations that will erode a good national structure. These are among the many things necessary for a successful nation.

The exaltation of a nation connotes things like an equitable economy, a good reputation, basically prowess in endeavours. There are certain nations whose reputation is marred as a result of the activities of its citizens. For others, their economies bear the direct negative consequences.

Any nation that seeks to be exalted will, first of all, have to exalt righteousness. In other words, that nation has to make it a custom to do the will of God. Every believer in Christ must believe, live, and teach this.

📖 Memory Verse

Righteousness exalts a nation, but sin is a disgrace to any people.

(Proverbs 14:34)
🙏 Prayer

Dear Jesus, I pray not only for myself but for my country: that seeds of righteousness will be planted; that they will be fruitful; that many trees of righteousness will be seen all over; that we will do what is required of us; and that we seek obedience every day. Amen.
