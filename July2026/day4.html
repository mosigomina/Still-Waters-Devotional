July 4

You Are in the World but Not of It

They are not of the world, just as I am not of the world. (John 17:16)

A plant grows from whatever it is rooted in. If you pull it from one soil and place it in another, eventually its leaves, its fruit, even its shape will tell you which soil won. This is the real meaning behind being in the world but not of it.

Being in the world but not part of it has nothing to do with where you live. Instead, it is all about what you are rooted in while you live there. Physically, all of us believers in Christ are present in every nation, every culture, every system around us, just as Jesus prayed His Father would not remove us from it (John 17:15). But the issue has always being source, not presence.

Look at what Romans 12:2 says: that the world we live in has a pattern, like a uniform it is always trying to force everybody into wearing. The Bible encourages us to refuse this uniform and to do so aggressively, constantly, never becoming tired enough to just conform.

What God gives us as an alternative is the gift of transformation, a renewing of the mind so thorough that, even while standing in the same soil as everyone else, what grows out of us looks unmistakably different.

Identity, therefore, has more to do with what is feeding our roots than where our feet are planted. To put it to the test, when there is clear disagreement between the world’s pattern and God’s word (whether on money, ambition, how to treat an enemy, what success means, and so on), which one moves you?

If the answer is always the pattern around you, the soil has already won, regardless of how the leaves look. If the answer is increasingly the Word and will of God, even when it costs you standing or comfort, then you are a true citizen of Christ’s kingdom, a true heir of God.

📖 Memory Verse

Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind.

(Romans 12:2)
🙏 Prayer

Dear God, let me be rooted in You even while I stand in this world. Renew my mind daily, so that when the world’s pattern and Your word disagree, it is Your word that moves me. Amen.
