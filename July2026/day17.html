July 17

Work for the Welfare of Your City

The Lord was with Joseph, so he became a successful man. And he was in the house of his master, the Egyptian… It came about that from the time he made him overseer in his house and over all that he owned, the Lord blessed the Egyptian’s house on account of Joseph; thus the Lord’s blessing was upon all that he owned, in the house and in the field. (Genesis 39:2-5)

Many are neglectful of the collective wellbeing of their communities. It is why we have scenarios of people looting grants that are supposed to be beneficial to the whole people, poorly erected structures that save costs but risk lives, and even littering that contributes to an unclean and unhealthy environment in the long run.

For Joseph, though he was sold as a slave, he worked. The fruit of this was the prosperity of all he did and the prosperity of Potiphar’s household. In a sense, the prosperity of Potiphar’s household was the prosperity of Joseph. In the same manner, the prosperity of the Israelites lied in the prosperity of their captor’s nation.

When it comes to the well-being and flourishing of a community, apathy and negligence are always the wrong approach. We can, in fact, find a biblical basis for this in God’s instruction to the Israelites He was sending into exile (Jeremiah 29:7). If they were expected to do that much for their captors, how much more should we do for our own nation.

The same is applicable to us. Let us be faithful believers heeding the instructions of our Lord. Let us actively seek the welfare of our communities and nations.

📖 Memory Verse

Seek the welfare of the city where I have sent you into exile, and pray to the Lord on its behalf; for in its welfare you will have welfare.

(Jeremiah 29:7)
🙏 Prayer

Dear Lord, teach me to look out for my city and grant me the grace to be diligent in my endeavours. I also pray that You will make prosperous the works done. Amen.
