July 25

We Must Obey God Rather Than Men

We ought to obey God rather than men. (Acts 5:29)

When the council ordered Peter and John to stop speaking about Jesus, they responded simply but clearly: we cannot but speak the things which we have seen and heard, they said. Take note that this was neither defiance for its own sake nor the invention of something with which to push back against authority. They were reporting what their own eyes and ears had already witnessed, and obeying the council’s order would have required denying that reality entirely.

This is a different kind of refusal than simple rebellion. More than anything, it is the recognition that some commands ask believers to unsee what they have already seen, to unknow what they already know, and that this is a line no instruction from any human authority can move.

Daniel was under the same pressure, as we Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. Saying no to the king, they were sentenced to their respective valleys of death: one to a den of lions, the others to a pit of flames. Were they people who wanted to disobey the king for the fun of it? No. But the command of the king contravened the command of God.

This is the line every believer eventually meets in some form, the moment a human instruction directly contradicts what God has already shown to be true. When that line appears, the answer should have already been settled long before the question was ever asked. Right?

📖 Memory Verse

We ought to obey God rather than men.

(Acts 5:29)
🙏 Prayer

Dear God, when human instruction asks me to deny what You have shown me to be true, give me the clarity and courage to obey You first. Amen.
