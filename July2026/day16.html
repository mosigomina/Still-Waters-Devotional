July 16

Live Peacefully with All Men

To make it your ambition and definitely endeavour to live quietly and peacefully, to mind your own affairs, and to work with your hands, as we charged you… (1 Thessalonians 4:11, AMPC)

A practical take on living peacefully with people acknowledges that there are people who still ruffle our feathers. These are the people that make it hard to maintain a peaceful life and especially make our outbursts and conflicts feel justifiable. For this, Scripture gives a solution.

The apostle Paul says in Romans 12:18 that as much as is up to you, live in peace with all men. The key phrase there is “as much as is up to you.”

We cannot control how another person behaves, but we are fully responsible for our own part in keeping peace. That means showing kindness, being gentle (even without compromise), being patient, and minding our own business. It also means communicating effectively, and setting and respecting boundaries, to mention a few. These form the foundation for true peace, the part that depends on us alone.

Sometimes, this requires a careful analysis of ourselves. Perhaps the way we respond, the jokes we say, or our indifference to situations because they do not affect us, all of these have a way of possibly setting us against others.

Peace is not just to be sought for in still waters but also in troubled waters. Even with all of these measures in place, there is still a possibility of being caught up in conflict, because we cannot determine how another person will react. Our part, however, is made clear to us in the Scriptures: that we must pursue being at peace with everyone, whatever they think of us.

📖 Memory Verse

If possible, so far as it depends on you, be at peace with all men.

(Romans 12:18)
🙏 Prayer

Lord Jesus, help me be committed to living peacefully with everyone, without bias. I know I let myself be kept on edge by the actions and speech of others so I ask that you grant me patience. I also ask that you help me in correcting every wrong character of mine that stands in the way. Amen.
