July 6

Pray for All People, Especially Leaders

I exhort therefore, that, first of all, supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks, be made for all men; for kings, and for all that are in authority. (1 Timothy 2:1-2)

In our Bible reference today, the Holy Spirit, through the apostle Paul, tells us why we need to pray for our leaders: so that we can live a quiet and peaceable life, in all godliness and honesty. In other words, our prayer for leaders is aimed at something we genuinely need, namely, the conditions of the very life we live under their governance. It is placed first, ahead of other requests, as though everything else we might ask for depends on getting this one right.

Did you know that this God-given duty does not wait for a leader to share our faith?

In Ezra 6:10, we are told of a pagan king that commanded that sacrifices be offered and prayers made for his own life and that of his sons, by priests serving a God he had no allegiance to himself. The instruction to pray for him still stood, and the priests still carried it out. Darius did not need to believe what we believe for our prayer over his life and his rule to stand with God.

And believers are not allowed to be mischievous and argue that the Lord did not tell us what to say in prayer. Here is Psalm 72:1-2 demonstrating that we can (and should) ask God to give the king justice and righteous judgment, so that he may defend the cause of the poor and judge the people rightly. Therefore, while we pray for the personal safety of leaders, we also pray that God directs them on caring for the people under them.

All of this rests on the fact that the king’s heart is in the hand of the Lord, like channels of water, turned wherever He directs (Proverbs 21:1). Whatever heart sits on whatever throne remains within reach of God.

📖 Memory Verse

The king’s heart is in the hand of the Lord, as the rivers of water: he turneth it whithersoever he will.

(Proverbs 21:1)
🙏 Prayer

Dear God, I bring those in authority over me before You today. Turn their hearts toward justice, especially for the vulnerable, and grant us the quiet, peaceable life Your word promises is possible. Amen.
