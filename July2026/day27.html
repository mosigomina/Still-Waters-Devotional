July 27

My Kingdom Is Not of This World

Therefore when Jesus perceived that they were about to come and take Him by force to make Him king, He departed again to the mountain by Himself alone. (John 6:15)

If you are a normal person, at some point in your life, you would desire influence, recognition, and success. Is this desire wrong or anti-God? Not necessarily so. But it hides a danger: it can quietly, very quietly, take the place of God in your heart.

The Lord Jesus knew this to be true. When Satan offered Him the kingdoms of this world, He refused. When the crowd of cheering and grateful people came to make Him king (mind you, they did not do it out of malice), the Lord Jesus withdrew Himself from them.

Why? Because being king is wrong? No; because it was outside the Father’s will for Him. It becomes even clearer when He stands before Pilate, declaring, “My kingdom is not of this world” (John 18:36).

Are we not the same? Do we not also, as believers in Christ Jesus, live in a world we do not belong to? Yes, we study, work, build families, serve our countries, and enjoy God’s blessings with gratitude. But none of these things is allowed to become our highest pursuit. After all, earthly kingdoms rise and fall; it is only God’s Kingdom that is designed to exist forever.

Let our hearts be set on our King; let us serve Him faithfully. While we function in different capacities in our nations, let us not forget that the earth is not our home.

📖 Memory Verse

Jesus answered, My kingdom is not of this world.

(John 18:36)
🙏 Prayer

Dear Lord, keep my heart fixed on Your Kingdom. Help me to use every earthly blessing wisely without allowing anything to take Your rightful place in my life. Amen.
