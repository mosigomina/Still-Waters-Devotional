July 8

Dominion Belongs to the Lord

For dominion belongeth unto the Lord: and he is the governor among the nations. (Psalm 22:28)

Some Bible verses have a melodious tone when we quote them, but they leave a bitter taste when we try to believe them. Psalm 22:28 is one such verse. Does it really say that dominion belongs to the Lord, and He is governor among the nations? Note that the nations being referenced here are not only the calm or stable ones. And the Lord is THE governor. Full stop.

Even if everybody believed this in the past, we would forgive Daniel if he refused. After all, his nation had been conquered, with his temple in ruins, his own future decided by the decree of a foreign king. By every visible measure available to him, dominion clearly belonged to Babylon.

Yet, in Daniel 4:17, we have the same man pronouncing judgment on Nebuchadnezzar himself, declaring that the Most High rules the kingdom of men and gives it to whomever He chooses, even to the lowest of men.

What did Daniel know that we are still struggling to hold onto?

If Daniel was reasoning from his present circumstances, he would have had nothing to stand on. Therefore, he would have reasoned from a longer memory, the record of how God had always raised up and torn down kings on His own terms, long before Babylon ever existed. Indeed, Daniel would have been living by the principle that God changes times and seasons, removes kings, and sets up kings, entirely on His own timing, regardless of how settled or shaken the throne currently appears (Daniel 2:21).

This is what our prayers are missing whenever they rise and fall with how strong or chaotic a season feels. We end up giving fear and attention to whatever looks unstoppable in the moment, rather than to the One who actually is. Daniel’s conviction did not wait for safer ground to stand on. Why should ours? Do we not have the same God? Does He, the Creator and Hegemon of the Ages, not wield the same wisdom and power as He did in the days of Daniel?

📖 Memory Verse

And he changes the times and the seasons: He removes kings, and sets up kings.

(Daniel 2:21)
🙏 Prayer

Dear God, I am reminded that no throne, no power, no chaos sits outside Your dominion. Let my prayers rise from confidence in who still governs, not fear of what I see. Amen.
