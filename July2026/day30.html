July 30

We Await the Saviour from Heaven

We also eagerly wait for the Saviour, the Lord Jesus Christ. (Philippians 3:20)

What does waiting for someone actually do to the way you live before they arrive?

For us believers in Christ, grace has appeared, teaching us to live soberly, righteously, and godly in the present age, while we wait for the blessed hope, the appearing of our great God and Saviour. Thank God for Scriptures like Titus 2:11–13 where we are taught that where others wait idly, our own waiting is instructive, even influencing how we live in the present rather than just giving us hope for later.

1 Thessalonians 1:10 makes the same point from a different angle. Paul commends a church for waiting for the Son from heaven, but the verses just before it describe a people known for their work of faith, labour of love, and reputation throughout the region. Were they sitting by, watching the sky? Or were they labouring while they waited, visibly demonstrated in how they lived among their neighbours?

This is the model for our duty to the nation. We are not citizens marking time until the Lord rescues us from this perverse age. We are citizens whose expectation of the Saviour produces a certain quality of life right now, soberness, integrity, visible good work, the kind 2 Peter 3:12–13 calls hastening the day by holy conduct.

The Saviour is coming to deliver us, yes. But while we wait, we do not stop being useful where we stand.

📖 Memory Verse

To wait for His Son from heaven... even Jesus.

(1 Thessalonians 1:10)
🙏 Prayer

Dear God, let my waiting for You be active. Let the hope of Your appearing be visible in how I live and labour today. Amen.
