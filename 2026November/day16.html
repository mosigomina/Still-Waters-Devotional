NOVEMBER 16

Sowing to the Spirit

Don’t be deceived: God is not mocked. For whatever a person sows he will also reap, because the one who sows to his flesh will reap destruction from the flesh, but the one who sows to the Spirit will reap eternal life from the Spirit. (Galatians 6:7–8)

There is a law at work in every believer called the law of sowing and reaping. According to the Apostle Paul, this is a law that applies to your inner life. Simply put, the law dictates that you are only allowed to gain strength from whatever you feed on. Therefore, if you sow to the flesh (through habits, entertainment, or thought patterns that ignore God), you will certainly reap weakness.

Romans 8:10–11 is not ambiguous. The argument is set forth: your energy for this race of life does not come from willpower. It comes from the Spirit who “quickens,” meaning that it is He who supplies the life required to finish. To use the words of the Lord Jesus, the flesh counts for nothing. The mind left unrenewed simply cannot sustain obedience no matter how hard it tries.

This is why many believers struggle with besetting sins. They try to conquer the flesh using the flesh. But flesh cannot cast out flesh. The only way to overcome is displacement: feeding the Spirit until His life becomes dominant.

To sow to the Spirit is to engage with the things of God, such as prayer, Scripture, worship, and fellowship. But you cannot employ these things as religious routines; you have to see and engage them as deposits of strength, as seeds that need to grow, resulting in a life that is very obviously a planting of the Lord.

This is why we sow to the Spirit as we invest time and effort in the things of God. This is why Galatians 6 warns us not to grow weary. To the soul that keeps sowing to the Spirit, God has already prepared grace, victory, and a customised prize. So, today, make it a must-do to deny the flesh and “feed” the Spirit with fresh attention.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Do not be deceived, God is not mocked; for whatever a man sows, this he will also reap.
(Galatians 6:7)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, teach me to sow rightly. Let the life of the Spirit overpower every weakness of the flesh. Amen.
