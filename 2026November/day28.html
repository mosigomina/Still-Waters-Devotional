NOVEMBER 28

Eternity

The end of all things is near; therefore, be alert and sober-minded for prayer. (1 Peter 4:7)

The illusion of permanence is an interesting issue. It refers to when something happens repeatedly and your mind begins to feel like it is always going to be that way or will always be that way. It makes a temporary experience feel permanent.

Dear believer, this psychological issue is what will make a person live as though there is no home outside this world, with no consciousness that one day all these repeated daily activities will end and there is a God to answer to.

Has it been a wonderful year for you? We hope so. We also hope that you bear in mind that this circle called Earth is not the end. As we enter and exit each year, we will not live here forever. This is not home, no matter how long we stay.

See each year God has gifted you as a chance to live for His purpose and acknowledge that you have been given borrowed time. Therefore, at the very top of your 2027 goals should be a plan for how you can add to the kingdom of God in your own way, with your own gifts, and how you can dismantle the works of darkness through your rebellion against sin and lukewarmness.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
…as we await the blessed hope and glorious appearance of our great God and Saviour, Jesus Christ.
(Titus 2:13)
🙏 PRAYER
Help us, Lord, to keep Your return in our hearts and keep us from falling until the day we are made perfect. In Jesus’ name.
