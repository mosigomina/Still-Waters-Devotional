NOVEMBER 30

A Thankful Heart

Let the whole earth shout triumphantly to the LORD! Serve the LORD with gladness; come before him with joyful songs. (Psalms 100:1–2)

Thanksgiving and gratitude come freely from a heart that acknowledges that life is only lived by the grace of God alone. The surprise element life tends to possess is the very reason why we need God. We don’t have eyes behind our heads, so it could never have been our carefulness that kept us.

The God we serve is all-knowing, sees all, and controls all by His sovereignty. We, as His children, are beneficiaries of His goodness and mercy, and He has kept us from evil all year round: from arrows that fly by day and by night, even while we sleep.The least we can do is praise, worship, and pray with thanksgiving, pouring our love on Him with songs, hymns, and words, because He deserves it.

Maybe you haven’t completed all your goals, even at this time. But surely you know that there is much more to thank God for. Surely you know that every moment of joy and salvation came about because He allowed it. Therefore, as we all prepare to wrap up 2026, let us keep records of the goodness and lovingkindness of our God, and go on thanking Him.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Let us come before His presence with thanksgiving; let us make a joyful noise to Him with songs of praise.
(Psalm 95:2)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, we thank You from the depths of our heart. We are grateful for Your love. Receive our praise, in Jesus’ name.
