NOVEMBER 22

Press On

I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith. There is reserved for me the crown of righteousness, which the Lord, the righteous Judge, will give me on that day, and not only to me, but to all those who have loved his appearing. (2 Timothy 4:7–8)

The Bible contains the stories of people whose lives continue to challenge us. The Apostle Paul is one such person. There are two things, among many others, we can see in his life.

One is that Paul refused to slip into that dangerous state of self-satisfaction and spiritual laziness that leads to spiritual decline: complacency. The other supplies an answer as to why: he was a hopeful man.

As believers, we have been admitted into the hope of the gospel upon our new birth. Presently, we have been united with Christ; hence, we are partakers of His death (and the death of our old nature) and His life (the birth of our new life), saved from the penalty and power of sin. After now, we will be partakers of His heavenly glory, resurrecting from death into an imperishable body (1 Corinthians 15:50–58).

But we must, as Paul did, forget what is behind and look forward to what is ahead, pressing on. We must refuse to hold on to our mistakes, repenting of them and renouncing them. We must give in to the transformative work of the Holy Spirit, letting Him produce the fruit of righteousness in us.

The Lord Jesus desires to let us into everlasting life (John 6:40). We must be encouraged to pursue holiness and do good works, both of which will become more desirable because of the newness of our nature, all while maintaining a vibrant hope in the second coming of the Lord, when we will receive our reward for a life well lived and a course well run.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Brothers, I do not consider myself yet to have taken hold of it. But one thing I do: forgetting what lies behind and reaching forward to what lies ahead, I press on toward the goal to win the prize of God’s heavenly calling in Christ Jesus.
(Philippians 3:13–14)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord Jesus, let the hope of the gospel be forever present in my heart, until Your coming or until my death. Amen.
