NOVEMBER 14

Standing to Run

And I questioned him further, “What are the two streams of the olive trees, from which the golden oil is pouring through the two golden conduits?” “These are the two anointed ones,” he said, “who stand by the Lord of the whole earth.” (Zechariah 4:12, 14)

In the vision of Zechariah, the reason the olive trees went on pouring oil was that they stood beside the Lord of all the earth. Their strength was not in how active they were. No, it was in their proximity to God.

The same truth runs through Hebrews 10. The reason we are able to endure is not that we have awesome willpower; it is that we are confident in the presence of God. And this is why, even though the race of life will overstretch everybody, the people who run the longest and the most beautifully are those who stand closest to God.

So, the first message today is that you protect your closeness to God.

The second message is that you do not try to serve God without first being sure of your standing with Him. You will exhaust yourself by so doing.

It is because of this that the Apostle Paul humbly confessed that “our sufficiency is of God” (2 Corinthians 3:5). In other words, even the power of ministry is not stored in us but received from Him.

You should know by now that what God rewards with grace is not frantic motion (going up and down) but disciplined stillness. Therefore, just as people might ask you how much you are willing to do for God, you should ask yourself how firmly you are planted beside Him. It is easier to bear fruit that please God this way.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Our sufficiency is of God.
(2 Corinthians 3:5)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, teach me to stand with You, that I may run with strength You supply. Amen.
