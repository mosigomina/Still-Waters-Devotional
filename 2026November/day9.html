NOVEMBER 9

The Collaborative Will

But flee from these things, you man of God, and pursue righteousness, godliness, faith, love, perseverance, and gentleness. Fight the good fight of faith; take hold of the eternal life to which you were called, and you made the good confession in the presence of many witnesses. (1 Timothy 6:11–12)

No one runs with God by accident. Though being talented is good, it does not guarantee destiny with God. Intention and determination are more useful here, which is why your will is a matter for discussion. It is why being God’s person is a matter of heart posture, where we make decisions that prove that we are surrendered to God in our hearts.

In Psalm 16:11, God shows the path of life. It is interesting that even though He shows it, He does not force anyone to walk on it. The only people God insists on are those who refuse to insist on their own ways. These are the people whose wills have become God’s landing place.

Take note, therefore, that God will not claim a person as His own and put that person to His great work until He is sure that that person’s will is parallel to His. With every decision you are going to make tomorrow, you show God whether His will for you is something you can yield to or something you will fight tooth and nail against.

Are you still praying for God’s plan while standing on your own will? Which runner argues with the track, the coach, or the referee? The grace of God does not flow that way. It flows and reaches you where and when your will begins to bow to Christ Jesus.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Not My will, but Yours be done.
(Luke 22:42)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, help me own my choices and align them with Your preferences, so I can be a man of God in posture and not just in name. Amen.
