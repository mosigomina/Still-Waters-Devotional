NOVEMBER 5

Back to the Drawing Board

I thought about my ways and turned my steps back to your decrees. (Psalm 119:59)

It is one of the abnormal normalcies of the practice of Christianity that some of us love to hear about God but are not motivated to do what He has commanded. For some other believers, doing God’s commands is not hard, but it must be done in the way they want. “What has God said, and how does God intend for His will to be carried out?” is a question we can easily avoid.

As we wrap up the year, it is a good time to reflect. Take a step back, look at what you are building, and see if it still tallies with the blueprint God gave you. It could be your marriage, your ministry, your business, or your life.

It is a time to reflect and ensure you are not running God’s vision in your own way, or building the wrong building on God’s land. It is advisable to take a step back at times like this: to re-evaluate and correct what needs correction.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
I considered my ways and turned my steps to Your testimonies.
(Psalm 119:59)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, we pray that You help us know Your will and walk in it with accuracy, in Jesus’ name.
