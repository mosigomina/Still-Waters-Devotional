NOVEMBER 10

What God Wants

“Father, if you are willing, take this cup away from me—nevertheless, not my will, but yours, be done” (Luke 22:42)

The secret to finishing anything is starting it. But it never ends at starting; starting well is just as important. If you start heading in the wrong direction, you will almost likely never reach the intended destination continuing like that.

Philosophical arguments abound as to what we are doing here on the earth and why there is even such a thing as life. Each of these set a person on a journey of discoveries, but because the starting point is skewed, unless God intervenes (and we are fortunate that He always intervenes), the end will be skewed.

In God’s book, the best starting point to any life of meaning is Christ Jesus. Guess what the destination is… Christ Jesus! And the way? Christ Jesus. Therefore, it is unsurprising when in Revelation 22:12–13, the Lord Himself declares that there is only One Who is both beginning and end—Him. To have our eyes (pursuit) and hearts (affection) fixed on the Author and Finisher of our faith is to be on the path to the right destination.

To fix our eyes and hearts on the Lord is to prize His work of salvation above everything else. It is to love and strive to emulate the perfect life that He lived while He walked on the earth, fully submitted and submitting to the will of the Father. It is to trust God to say, even with the threat of death before our very eyes, “Father, not my will but Yours be done.”

This is how to live a meaningful life. It is to yield everything to God and disregard whatever anyone else says. Human expectations abound, yes, but let us only do what God wants.

📖 MEMORY VERSE

(Proverbs 3:5–6)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, help me trust and submit to Your will above all else. Amen.
