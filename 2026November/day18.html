NOVEMBER 18

The Might of Presence

Then the angel of the LORD appeared to him and said: “The LORD is with you, valiant warrior.” ... The LORD turned to him and said, “Go in the strength you have and deliver Israel from the grasp of Midian. Haven’t I sent you?” (Judges 6:12, 14)

The story of Gideon tells us many interesting things about how God sees reality. The first time God met the man, He called him mighty (Judges 6:12). Two verses later, He said, “Go in this your might.” God was referring to His own presence. In His eyes, that presence was Gideon’s weapon.

Clearly, God did not want Gideon afraid. Fear is misplaced worship. It elevates problems until they become the centre of our attention. But when God stands with a person and that person is aware of His presence, that person can go in the might of that presence and every bastard fear will vamoose!

No wonder, when the Lord was about to depart in Matthew 28, He left a promise: “I am with you always.” This is the true might behind His mission for us. The believer is not allowed to use fear or self-confidence as fuel for destiny. You must be confident in the One who has called you. The more conscious you are of Him, the less room fear has to breathe.

So, when anxiety rises, the answer is not to stare harder at the challenge. The answer is to run deeper into God.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
And surely I am with you always, to the very end of the age.
(Matthew 28:20)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, make Your presence my might. Let fear be silenced by the One who stands beside me. Amen.
