NOVEMBER 21

Recount the Promises

By these He has given us very great and precious promises, so that through them you may share in the divine nature, escaping the corruption that is in the world because of evil desire. (2 Peter 1:4)

The Bible was written for several purposes, including to ensure that future generations have proof of the workings and track record of God. It might not make much sense when someone hears David say, “With the Lord is great mercy,” until we see what he has gone through and how the Lord showed him mercy.

In God’s word is His promise, and because we know that God cannot lie, we can hold on to every word He says. It is easy to drop out of a race when the end is not in view, but when through faith we see what is set before us, we will run.

So, recount the promises. Tell your heart the tales.

Say to your soul, “Why are you discouraged? Hope in the One who answers prayers.” The God with whom we have dealings is not one to disappoint, so we can stake our lives upon His Word. This is how to keep your heart and faith from failing.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
So that by two unchangeable things [His promise and His oath] in which it is impossible for God to lie, we who have fled [to Him] for refuge would have strong encouragement and indwelling strength to hold tightly to the hope set before us.
(Hebrews 6:18 AMP)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, in that day when my heart is weary, cause me to remember Your faithfulness.
