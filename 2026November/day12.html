NOVEMBER 12

Be an Imitator

Therefore, be imitators of God, as dearly loved children… (Ephesians 5:1)

The first two chapters of the Bible provide us with a picture that introduces us to God’s intent for man: “Let Us make man in Our image, according to Our likeness” (Genesis 1:26). After man Fell, this likeness became warped, as man became a caricature of what he was never intended to be.

How wonderful that the Lord Jesus’ death provided a way for us to embody the truth and example of God yet again. The groundwork for following God’s example is now a relationship with Him by adoption through Christ Jesus.

Biblically, imitation goes far beyond external copying. It is a Spirit-empowered conformity of belief, character, and conduct to an approved pattern, which is primarily God, and then mature believers and churches that mirror Him.

The Lord Jesus Himself taught that we must follow the example of God (Matthew 5:44–48). The chapter ends with a simple instruction: “Be perfect just as your heavenly Father is perfect.”

What we are called into is indeed a high standard. But we are called into it, empowered by His Spirit and with access to grace. Imitation is the result of the Holy Spirit dwelling in us, shaping our hearts and lives.

What this means is that we truly let the Holy Spirit work in us, even as we desire to live like Christ. When He does, we see people, including our enemies, as worthy of love and mercy (Luke 10:27–37). We become cautious in our speech, as well as in every other area of conduct.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Be perfect, therefore, as your heavenly Father is perfect.
(Matthew 5:48)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, I thank You for the sacrifice of Your Son. Grant me the grace to conform to the work of the Spirit in me, so I will bear Your image more and more each day. Amen.
