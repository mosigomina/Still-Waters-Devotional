NOVEMBER 13

Forgiving Ignorance

Therefore, with your minds ready for action, be sober-minded and set your hope completely on the grace to be brought to you at the revelation of Jesus Christ. As obedient children, do not be conformed to the desires of your former ignorance. But as the one who called you is holy, you also are to be holy in all your conduct; for it is written, “Be holy, because I am holy.” (1 Peter 1:13–16)

Simply defined, ignorance is the lack of understanding, information, or knowledge about a thing. It may not be the same as foolishness but they are definitely close companions. The sincere but ignorant civilian who crosses a military “no trespass” zone will be sincerely dealt with.

Therefore, teaching and discipleship in the truth of God’s Word cannot be overemphasised. Both of these deliver us from poor judgement and bad decision-making.

The consequences of ignorance are very similar, even equal, to those of foolishness. Unlike the ignorant who do not know any better, the foolish one knows better but refuses to do it (Proverbs 1:7).

The fact that you are reading this means you are interested in living today by the wisdom that comes from the Word of God (which is the ultimate wisdom). As the year comes to an end, you might have broken some, most, or all of the resolutions you made for the year. Maybe you even stumbled into besetting sins along the way. We have an Advocate with the Father—Jesus the Righteous (1 John 2:1–2).

It is very important that one repents (Acts 17:30) by a change of heart and a change in one’s way of life (metanoia), learning from the most perfect life that ever lived, that is, Jesus. Do not dwell on past mistakes and on the ignorance that made you fail. Now that you know better, move on to holiness and righteousness.

You should also ensure you forgive those who have offended you in their ignorance. Ask the merciful Father to forgive them, for they do not know what they do.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Then Jesus said, “Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they do.”
(Luke 23:34)
🙏 PRAYER
Pray to God to help you be holy as He is holy, merciful as He is merciful, and full of grace towards others, in Jesus’ Name.
