NOVEMBER 24

Set Your Affection Above

Don’t store up for yourselves treasures on earth, where moth and rust destroy and where thieves break in and steal. But store up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust destroys, and where thieves don’t break in and steal. For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also. (Matthew 6:19–21)

There is a famous movie character who found an item which he then named “my precious.” He became so consumed by his love for that item that he was willing to do anything and everything to ensure it was his always—even die with it when it was destroyed.

In today’s Bible reference, the Lord Jesus paints a picture for us. The best of what Earth has to offer can be lost, stolen, and even destroyed. But everything Heaven offers (our glorification, the end of suffering, and eternal fellowship, to name a few) has no end. We will never feel the pain of the loss of those things. Not when we are here, for we are assured of our inheritance by God Himself (Acts 20:32), and certainly not when we get there. That which is above is everlasting.

And so, it is this that the Apostle Paul, speaking in his epistle to the Colossians, asks us to set our thoughts upon. He urges us that, as we wonder at and ponder what is to come, we should no longer live as we once did: earthly-constrained, with no anticipation of the blessedness that is to come.

With life on earth comes gold and silver, yes, but they perish eventually. With life on earth comes the temptation of believers to worry. Remember: what is above is eternal, so fill your hearts with thoughts of these things.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Therefore, since you have been raised with Christ, strive for the things above, where Christ is seated at the right hand of God. Set your minds on things above, not on earthly things.
(Colossians 3:1–2)
🙏 PRAYER
Dear Lord, ever so often I am tempted to live for myself and pursue all that the world has to offer me. Help me remember that at the end of this is death. Help me to choose You always and all You have in store for me.
