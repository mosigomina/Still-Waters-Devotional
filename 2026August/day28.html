AUGUST 28

The Banquet Restored

“Blessed are those who are called to the marriage supper of the Lamb.” (Revelation 19:9)

Project management is currently a buzzword. Everybody is learning that being organised means working with timelines and deliverables. Except that when a human being plans something, that plan can be disrupted, whether by budget cuts, delays, or mistakes. God is different.

The master schedule of God is so unimaginable it beats time, scope, and disruption. Apart from how the timeline stretches far past the beginning of time and into eternity, even the greatest disruptors in human history (sin and death) could not threaten it. So unshakeable is God’s timeline that even now, the most morally corrupt human being on earth can submit to it and find a seat at God’s banquet of restoration.

Revelation 19:6–9 lets us peek at the ultimate deliverable in God’s grand design: the Marriage Supper of the Lamb. This is where we find that God has scheduled a final, incorruptible feast that nothing can stop. No earthly crisis, no human failure, no physical death.

All of this is written to remind us that whereas human plans collapse under pressure, God’s plan moves history steadily forward toward this singular event. With the great feast already prepared and the Lord of All already seated at the head of the table, the question is worth sitting with: are you fit to participate? Will you wear the wedding robe assigned to you, or ignore it for one you made for yourself?

📖 MEMORY VERSE
“Let us be glad and rejoice and give Him glory, for the marriage of the Lamb has come, and His wife has made herself ready.”
(Revelation 19:7)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, let nothing keep me from Your banquet. I receive the robe You have prepared and take my seat at the table You have set. Amen.
