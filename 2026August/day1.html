AUGUST 1

The Problem of Empty Hands

“If only the Lord had killed us back in Egypt,” they moaned. “There we sat around pots filled with meat and ate all the bread we wanted. But now you have brought us into this wilderness to starve us all to death.” (Exodus 16:3)

You will agree with me that the very mountain you are trying to scale is not the first problem you have ever encountered. This also means you have had victories before, whether in examinations, health challenges, job-related situations, or other areas of life.

The time when the profane Esau gave his birthright to Jacob was not the first time he had been hungry and was fed. Yet, he exchanged his blessing for a plate of food (Hebrews 12:16).

The problem of empty hands is forgetfulness. People tend to forget the greatness of God in moments of distress, and such forgetfulness is unrighteous. The Israelites wasted no time murmuring against God because they were hungry, even after witnessing all the miracles He had performed for them (Exodus 16:1–3).

The Bible is full of accounts of God’s greatness, showing how He turned nothing into abundance. It was Jehovah Jireh who provided the ram in place of Isaac. It was also He who multiplied the loaves and the fishes.

You, too, have had experiences with God that displayed His greatness. Therefore, whenever you find yourself in lack or distress, labour to remember. If He did it before, He will do it again.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
And my God shall supply all your need according to His riches in glory by Christ Jesus.
(Philippians 4:19)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, when my hands feel empty, remind me of every time You have filled them before. Let my memory of Your faithfulness be stronger than my present fear. Amen.
