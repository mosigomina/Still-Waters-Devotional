AUGUST 27

Restoration with Scars

“The glory of this latter temple shall be greater than the former, says the Lord of hosts. And in this place I will give peace.” (Haggai 2:9)

Among the many things we associate with glory, shame is rarely one of them. By our own estimation, beauty leaves the room with every scar that comes in, even though our natural forefathers made tribal marks a thing of beauty. Generally, the reverse is often true. And this is why it is such an awesome thing that even where there are flaws, God can pull out glory and overlay them with it.

We see this very clearly in Haggai 2. Having seen how their labour amounted to very little (verse 3), they were saddened. And this is how it can be with a believer in Christ. All our efforts spent praying, asking God to let light radiate from us because of the shame from our disobedience, can look small. As if nothing is happening.

God’s greatness shines through in the way He restores. In Haggai, He told them that although the temple they had built was small, made with wood, seemingly easily smashed apart, He would fill it with glory.

And that is what the Lord is saying today: although our present lives might still bear cracks from our old life before we came to Christ, God is able to seal up these cracks and fill in the gaps with His glory.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
“And Thomas answered and said to Him, ‘My Lord and my God!’”
(John 20:28)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, where my life still bears cracks and scars, fill them with Your glory. Let what remains of my old life become the very place where Your greatness is most clearly seen. Amen.
