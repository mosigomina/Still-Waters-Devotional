AUGUST 14

The Deaf Who Could Not Hear

“Therefore we must pay much closer attention to what we have heard, lest we drift away from it.” (Hebrews 2:1)

Did you know that God sometimes waits for things to be quiet before He speaks?

In Mark 7, we see how He healed the deaf man. He took the man aside, far from the crowd and noise, then spoke one word, Ephphatha, and what had been shut for years opened completely.

Is it possible that God has spoken to you but His voice and words got lost in the rowdiness of other voices and other words? Maybe the voices of work and worry? Maybe the voices of human opinion and obligation?

The Bible teaches that since the beginning of things, God has been speaking. He speaks through Scripture, through His Spirit and presence, through the conviction born from listening to His word and instructions. The problem is our attentiveness.

What has God been saying to you that you have not created time for listening and meditation? If you are willing, God is even happy to create space for you, as he did with the deaf man. But if you are not willing, you will remain distracted, and the potent words of God for healing and salvation will drift away.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
“Therefore we must pay much closer attention to what we have heard, lest we drift away from it.”
(Hebrews 2:1)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, lead me away from the noise today; open what has been shut in me, and let me hear clearly what You have been saying. Amen.
