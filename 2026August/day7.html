AUGUST 7

Rest in His Provision

Come to Me, all you who labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take My yoke upon you and learn from Me, for I am gentle and lowly in heart, and you will find rest for your souls. For My yoke is easy and My burden is light. (Matthew 11:28–30)

Imagine waking up every morning with no food stored in your house, no savings to depend on, and no idea where your next meal would come from. This was the reality of the Israelites in the wilderness. They had left Egypt, but they had not yet entered the Promised Land. Every single day, they depended completely on God.

Each morning, God sent manna from heaven. They gathered only what they needed for that day because God wanted them to learn to trust Him daily. Then something different happened. On the sixth day, God instructed them to gather twice as much food because the seventh day was to be a Sabbath, a day of rest. Normally, if they kept manna overnight, it would spoil. But this time, it remained fresh exactly as God had promised.

God was teaching them that His provision could be trusted. They did not have to spend the Sabbath worrying about what they would eat or searching for more manna. They could rest because God had already provided for them.

This raises an important question for us today: Can we safely stop striving and trust what God has promised?

Rest is an expression of faith, believing that God will remain faithful to us. If He has promised to care for us, we can live free from fear and doubt.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
Come to Me, all you who labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.
(Matthew 11:28)
🙏 PRAYER
Teach me to find rest in Your promises.
