AUGUST 19

Out of the Wilderness

“Then Jesus was led by the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted by the devil. After fasting forty days and forty nights, he was hungry.” (Matthew 4:1–2)

With the children of Israel, God decided that the wilderness would be where they would be humbled and tested after they incurred His anger. It was discipline, but it was not abandonment.

In the places where we tend to feel the most alone or even stagnated, the Lord is there with us and He is working.

What the Israelites and Jesus have in common regarding their wilderness experience is that both had a pronouncement from heaven and yet encountered the wilderness afterward. The Israelites had a promise of a land flowing with milk and honey but had become wanderers. Jesus was to redeem mankind from sin, yet He was facing great temptation.

In Jesus’s time in the wilderness, though tempted and tested, He was able to call to mind the Word of the Lord, indicating allegiance in spite of His current situation. In our own circumstances, dry and wearing as they may sometimes be, bear in mind that the Lord has you. Jesus knew this, and it was why He was able to prevail.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
“Remember how the Lord your God led you all the way in the wilderness these forty years, to humble and test you in order to know what was in your heart, whether or not you would keep His commands.”
(Deuteronomy 8:2)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, I will continue to call to mind and place hope in Your great love and faithfulness, Your unfailing compassions that have not let me be consumed. Help me remember these now and always. Amen.
