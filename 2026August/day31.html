AUGUST 31

The Firstborn from the Dead

“But now Christ is risen from the dead, and has become the firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep.” (1 Corinthians 15:20)

Theologians have debated seriously what Jesus Christ was doing in the grave for three days. But across every position, everybody agrees that the Lord occupied death the way a conqueror occupies enemy territory, totally and completely bringing it under authority.

When asked what we fear the most, some people are quick to say poverty, or rejection, or failure. What about death? What about the seeming finality of it, the way it forces even the liveliest person to go quiet and rotten in a matter of hours?

Therefore, when the angels asked those women in Luke 24:5, “Why do you seek the living among the dead?”, it was to challenge their assumption that death had swallowed another human being. In reality, the Lord Jesus broke death’s authority held over every human being since Adam. And He is still doing that today.

The same Jesus who called the dead back to life and walked out of the grave Himself is the same Jesus who is brother and Lord to all who believe in Him. Having conquered death, He teaches us to do the same. This is why He is called the firstborn from the dead.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
“Why do you seek the living among the dead? He is not here, but is risen.”
(Luke 24:5)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, whatever has me sealed in and silent, You have already conquered it from the inside. Let me live today like someone whose grave has an exit. Amen.
