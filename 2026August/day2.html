AUGUST 2

Enough for Today

“Behold, I will rain bread from heaven for you. And the people shall go out and gather a certain quota every day, that I may test them, whether they will walk in My law or not.” (Exodus 16:4)

Protection is one of the principles of financial literacy, alongside saving, investing, and budgeting. These principles are taught to maximise present income, protect resources, and prevent financial ruin during unexpected emergencies. This is wisdom that prepares for the future as much as it provides for the present.

While these principles are sound and responsible, it is worth viewing them through the lens of the Lord’s Prayer in Matthew 6:11: “Give us this day our daily bread.”

The first lesson is faith. God desires to be involved in our daily provision. Whether you have a storehouse filled with a month’s or even a year’s worth of supplies, your confidence that you will be provided for each day should rest in God alone. Remember, every day has enough trouble of its own (Matthew 6:34), and a single day can bring circumstances that make years of provision disappear.

The second lesson is freedom from anxiety. God desires to remove your anxiety, and this is made explicit in Scripture. You are instructed to replace your worry about the future with thanksgiving and prayer in the present (Philippians 4:6).

When anxiety begins to creep into your heart, cast all your cares on Him by bringing your requests before Him, for He cares for you (1 Peter 5:7).

Finally, consider the love of God. If earthly parents, despite their imperfections, willingly sacrifice their comfort to provide for their children, how much more will your heavenly Father care for you? Reflect on the depth of His love for you today as His child.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
“Give us this day our daily bread.”
(Matthew 6:11)
🙏 PRAYER
Father, teach me to trust You for today, and to bring my anxieties to You rather than carry them alone. You are my provider. Amen.
