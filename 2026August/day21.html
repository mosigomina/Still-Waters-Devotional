AUGUST 21

Set Free to Serve

“Let my people go, so that they may worship me.” (Exodus 7:16)

As believers in Christ Jesus, our service to God is an active response to His grace and stands on that foundation alone. Service is a by-product of faith, and the possibility of being known as servants of God is a result of what Christ has done on the cross, delivering us from slavery to sin and binding us in service to Jesus. Paul understood this, which is why he described himself as a bondservant of Jesus Christ. The dynamic of our relationship with God is Father-child, Lover-beloved, and also Master-servant, and Jesus emphasises this through various parables.

A part of the labour the Lord requires of us is that we tell of Him—what He has done and what He is able to do in the lives of men. For the sake of the kingdom, we are to extend bridges of salvation and the accurate knowledge of it that is found in Christ Jesus.

The grace of God toward us should produce fruit. Like Paul, we strive to be who we are called to be in Christ, labourers in His kingdom. Blessed is the servant the Master finds doing what is required of him when the Master returns (Luke 12:42–43).

📖 MEMORY VERSE
“But by the grace of God I am what I am, and His grace toward me was not in vain; but I laboured more abundantly than they all, yet not I, but the grace of God which was with me.”
(1 Corinthians 15:10)
🙏 PRAYER
Dear Lord, thank You for calling me out of darkness into Your light of salvation. Help me to make full use of the grace that is able to work in me. Amen.
