AUGUST 15

Breaking Identity Chains

“The Spirit Himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God.” (Romans 8:16)

As believers in Christ and students of the Bible, we know that choices are keys to destiny. We also know that the world does not wait for us to be wise or strong before attaching to us defining identities, which are actually chains in disguise.

These identities might be tribal, or derived from family history, or defined by economic background, even cultural prescriptions. What they are not is neutral or harmless. They inform you of your worth, of what to expect out of life, of what is possible for you. They are powerful because everyone around you might be wearing them too.

Except that God is against them. Except that the Bible teaches that His intention is to redeem (purchase and claim) us from every tribe, tongue, people, and nation.

Therefore, the blood of Christ was shed, acquiring for every one of us a new identity. Therefore, the Holy Spirit was sent to bear witness that we are children of God (Romans 8:16).

No greater bondage exists than that of a person’s identity. In fact, it is because an individual has not been born again and therefore is not a child of God that they end up in hell.

Let us embrace only the identity that God has granted us, acknowledging His great wisdom and power. Let us leave behind background, bloodline, and everything else that might short-circuit God’s stake and power in our lives.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
“The Spirit Himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God.”
(Romans 8:16)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, let the blood of Jesus speak louder than every name I was given before I knew You. I receive the identity Your Spirit confirms (that of a child of God) and nothing less. Amen.
