AUGUST 5

The Desperate Outsider

“And other sheep I have which are not of this fold; them also I must bring, and they will hear My voice; and there will be one flock and one shepherd.” (John 10:16)

The Lord with whom we have dealings never turns away a penitent heart, nor is anyone too unworthy to come before Him. He has demonstrated this throughout Scripture and ultimately confirmed it by sending His Son into the world.

The sinner, the weak, the hopeless, and the broken can all look to Him without fear of being put to shame. Whoever seeks Him with a sincere and wholehearted heart will find Him, for He delights in revealing Himself to those who earnestly seek Him.

His power to save reaches to the uttermost, extending beyond every depth of sin, failure, and despair. There is no broken life given to Him that cannot be restored.

Therefore, trust Him when He says, “Come.” Run upon the waters at His command, fixing your eyes on Him. The One who calls you is faithful, and He will never fail those who trust in Him.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
“Come to Me, all you who labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.”
(Matthew 11:28)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, draw those who are without closer to You. Let all men see You and desire You wholeheartedly. Amen.
