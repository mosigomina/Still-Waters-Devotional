AUGUST 12

Healing and Forgiveness Together

“Who forgiveth all thine iniquities; who healeth all thy diseases.” (Psalm 103:3)

Most people come to God for one thing at a time, like heal my body, fix my situation, restore my losses, remove my fears. And God, in His mercy, always responds. But in Psalm 103:3, we see that forgiveness and healing are listed together, in the same breath, as though separating them was never God’s intention. In other words, He forgives; He heals; and in that order.

When four men cut through a roof to lower their paralysed friend before the Lord Jesus, the room was expecting a healing. What Jesus said first was: “Son, your sins are forgiven.” The crowd was confused, understandably: we are doing legs today, Lord, not our ledger of misbehaviours and wrongdoings. But what the Lord was doing was what mattered most. What’s the point of fixing legs if all they do is hurry us into hell?

While there are doctors to treat the body and counsellors to patch the mind, God reaches into the heart and soul, smashes apart the foundations of sin and sickness, and expunges the written aftereffects.

God’s way is to make a person whole, spirit, soul, and body. That is why He starts with forgiveness, fixing us from the inside.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
“… and none there will say, “I am sick.” The people who dwell there will be forgiven their iniquity.”
(Isaiah 33:24)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, I come to You for more than a fix. Deal with what is broken in my soul, and let the healing that follows be complete. Amen.
