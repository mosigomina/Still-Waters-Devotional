AUGUST 18

The Enemy’s Defeat

“I have given you authority to trample on snakes and scorpions and to overcome all the power of the enemy; nothing will harm you.” (Luke 10:19)

The destruction of Pharaoh’s army at the Red Sea was more than Israel’s deliverance. More than anything, it was the public humiliation of a defeated enemy. The same waters that became a pathway for God’s people became the instrument of judgment for those who pursued them. In an even greater way, the cross of Christ exposed the limits of Satan’s power. Through His death and resurrection, Jesus stripped the powers of darkness of their ultimate weapon and proved that God’s redemptive purpose could never be overthrown.

When the seventy disciples returned rejoicing that even demons submitted to them, Jesus reminded them that He had already seen Satan fall like lightning from heaven. Their authority over evil spirits was evidence that the kingdom of God had already begun to dismantle the enemy’s dominion. The cross completed that decisive victory, revealing that Satan’s power was always subject to the authority of Christ. Every triumph over darkness points back to Calvary, where the enemy’s strength was exposed before the wisdom and power of God.

The enemy has not ceased to oppose God’s people, but he fights as a defeated foe. Our confidence therefore rests in the finished work of Jesus, who has already unveiled the enemy’s defeat and secured our place in His triumph.
