AUGUST 24

A Future and a Hope

“For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, says the Lord, thoughts of peace and not of evil, to give you a future and a hope.” (Jeremiah 29:11)

Can a crippled man walk? Can a slave go free? Can the sick recover? Can a sinner be redeemed?

The answer the Scriptures give is a resounding yes! Blind Bartimaeus received his sight. Joseph stepped out of a prison cell to become second-in-command over Egypt. Moses fled into the desert as a fugitive and returned to liberate a nation. Lazarus walked out of a tomb after four days in the grave at the sound of a single name.

The woman with the issue of blood suffered for twelve years. She spent every penny she had on doctors who failed her. She lived with social isolation, constant pain, and absolute exhaustion. Then she met Jesus. Her hope returned, and her life was restored.

God never gives up on people. He stands firmly on His word. Jeremiah 29:11 was originally spoken to exiles sitting in a destroyed city with no visible future. Yet God declared over them: I know the plans I have for you—plans of peace, a future, and a hope.

Job 14:7 states it clearly: there is hope for a tree that is cut down; it will sprout again, and its new shoots will not fail.

God can rebuild what looks entirely ruined.
