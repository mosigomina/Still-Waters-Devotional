AUGUST 20

The Danger of an Empty House

“When an unclean spirit goes out of a man, he goes through dry places, seeking rest, and finds none. Then he says, ‘I will return to my house from which I came.’ And when he comes, he finds it empty, swept, and put in order.” (Matthew 12:43–44)

A common self-improvement principle is that when abandoning stunting habits, it helps to find other activities to replace them. Out with the bad, in with the good.

Matthew 12 tells us a similar story. An important lesson to draw from it is that we are expected to begin filling the space that has been vacated. Concerning demonic possession or bondage to sinful habits, God has delivered us so that we will no longer be slaves to what seeks to master us. Yet no matter how clean and put in order a new life may be, the enemy always lies in wait looking for opportunity.

Our constant solution as found in Christ Jesus is to be full of God. Being full in this context is indicative of an overwhelming presence that influences everything around it. As believers, we have the Spirit of Christ dwelling in us, and we are expected to move by the empowerment and enablement His Spirit provides toward keeping God’s commandments. The end result of a man who refuses to fill himself with God can be a worse state than he was in before.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
“Do not get drunk on wine, which leads to debauchery. Instead, be filled with the Spirit.”
(Ephesians 5:18)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, I pray that You will help me commit to filling myself with You daily, learning Your words and commandments, so that Your presence in my life will be a daily influence. Amen.
