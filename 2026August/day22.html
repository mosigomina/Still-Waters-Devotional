AUGUST 22

The Broken Covenant

“The Lord turned and looked straight at Peter. Then Peter remembered the word the Lord had spoken to him: ‘Before the rooster crows today, you will disown me three times.’ And he went outside and wept bitterly.” (Luke 22:61–62)

Can you imagine the cold realisation that hit the Apostle Peter the moment the rooster crowed and Jesus looked at him? Scripture says he went outside and wept, showing how deeply the guilt struck him. Guilt can be an indicator of a conscience that is alive. It is the persistence of it, unaddressed, that becomes problematic.

God stands ready to forgive us if we come to Him in faith and earnest repentance. When eyes are opened to our wrongs and hearts are struck by the heaviness of guilt, the path to restoration is an approach toward God in repentance. The Lord is a loving Father who stands ready to receive the one who turns to Him in brokenness. David expressed it rightly in Psalm 51:17—a broken and contrite heart, He will not despise.

It is because of the richness of God’s grace that scriptures like Romans 6:1 are written. While it serves as a caution, it is also a window into the character of God: merciful, patient, and abounding in love.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
“The Lord is compassionate and gracious, slow to anger, abounding in love.”
(Psalm 103:8)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord Jesus, in the days I fall short, help me lean into Your abounding mercy. Grant me the boldness to stand before You and seek Your forgiveness, for it is in coming to You that I find peace. Amen.
