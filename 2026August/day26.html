AUGUST 26

The Second Chance

“Simon, son of Jonah, do you love Me?” (John 21:17)

As we saw yesterday, the Lord is full of mercy and constant love (Psalm 103:8). Mercy and truth go before Him (Psalm 89:14). He showed mercy to the people of Nineveh when He sent Jonah to declare His judgment on them. When they repented, He relented, pardoned them, and showed His mercy (Jonah 3). Truly, He is a God that gives second chances.

Think about King Manasseh who did evil in the sight of God, but was restored to his throne after he repented in captivity (2 Kings 21). Or Peter who denied the Lord Jesus Christ three times with cursing and swearing, but the Lord reinstated him after the resurrection (Matthew 26:69–75, John 21:15–17).

Building on how God is able to rebuild the ruins of our lives, we are saying today that God’s heart is large enough to give us second chances when we mess up. At the risk of pushing it too far, one might even say that until one passes from this side of eternity into the next, these second chances are infinite.

Which is not at all to say that we should live reckless lives, disregard the grace and graciousness of God. However, let us be assured that the One we follow will not discard us at the smallest inconvenience or error. As long as we are interested in Him, willing to receive all that He has given, inheritances and instructions, He will continue to give second chances.

📖 MEMORY VERSE
“Yahweh is a compassionate and gracious God, slow to anger and rich in faithful love and truth, maintaining faithful love to a thousand generations, forgiving wrongdoing, rebellion, and sin.”
(Exodus 34:6)
🙏 PRAYER
Oh God our Father, look upon us with Your mercy and give us the grace to do Your will and to please You at all times. Amen.
