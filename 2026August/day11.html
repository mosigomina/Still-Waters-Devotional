AUGUST 11

The Long-Term Sufferer

“Daughter, your faith has made you well. Go in peace, and be healed of your affliction.” (Mark 5:34)

Twelve years is long enough to stop expecting, long enough for a condition to become something you have gotten used to carrying rather than bring to God. Against such an outcome, won’t you be the outlier? Won’t you be like the woman in Mark 5 who, even after spending everything she had on her illness, still pressed against the crowd to meet the Lord?

One of the harshest effects of long suffering is imposed silence. It is a type of quietness that resembles peace externally. In truth, it is resignation, submitting to the pain, just hoping to be able to manage it, without knowing that this is eating at faith to believe in God’s intervention.

Thank God for that woman who continued to believe! The Bible teaches that when she heard that the Lord was passing, she kept saying to herself that she only needs a single genuine contact. And didn’t God honour this faith?

For His children, God’s power does not fluctuate, neither does His willingness to help. Just never stop believing. This is the faith that makes well.
