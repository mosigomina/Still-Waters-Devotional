AUGUST 13

The Blind Who Cried Out

“And Jesus said to him, ‘Go your way; your faith has made you well.’ And immediately he received his sight.” (Mark 10:52)

Yesterday’s devotional focused on the woman who did not stop believing. Today’s focuses on Bartimaeus, the man who did not stop crying out, loudly, persistently, past every voice telling him to be quiet, until the Lord Jesus stopped.

What made Bartimaeus’ cry effective was its precision. When Jesus asked him what he wanted, Bartimaeus gave a specific answer. There was no vagueness in his request, from which we can learn that God meets us at the point of honest asking.

Having being taught to humble ourselves before God, we might cultivate a habit of dressing up our requests or softening them into things we consider more acceptable. This is good, but it can also come across as hypocrisy or double-mindedness.

If there is something specific you know you need, which God has promised to supply, you really don’t have to circle it, hoping that God will fill in what you have left unnamed. It is a different matter if you are not sure, but even that can be submitted as uncertainty in clear terms.

Take your time to think before you pray. You don’t have to be cunning with your requests, nor hesitant. God is not distant from His children. Ask, He says, and you will receive what you asked for, and your joy will be full.

🙏 PRAYER
Lord, I will say plainly what I need. I want to see. Open my eyes to whatever I have been looking past, and let my asking be as honest as Bartimaeus was. Amen.
