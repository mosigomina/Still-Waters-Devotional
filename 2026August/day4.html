AUGUST 4

Grumbling and Gratitude

“Shall we receive good from God, and shall we not receive evil?” (Job 2:10)

Grumbling is the act of complaining, often over minor or recurring issues. It is an attitude that God consistently frowned upon throughout Scripture. A grumbling heart easily overlooks God’s countless acts of goodness and instead fixates on what seems lacking. In doing so, it subtly suggests that He is unwise or unfair for withholding certain things we expect from Him.

Gratitude acknowledges God both in seasons of peace and in seasons of storm. It recognises that if He has done it in times past, He is surely able to do it again. We are called to live each day clothed in thanksgiving, confident that the One who owns us is faithfully working all things together for our good.

It is easy to notice the hardships and disappointments of life. Christianity calls us to deliberately choose gratitude in every circumstance, counting our blessings and never forgetting them. As the Apostle Paul wrote, “Rejoice in the Lord always. Again I will say, rejoice.”

📖 MEMORY VERSE
“I will bless the Lord at all times; His praise shall continually be in my mouth.”
(Psalm 34:1)
🙏 PRAYER
Lord, help me to always see You in every circumstance, and to rejoice in all things. Amen.
